# Push-based change propagation in Set-Based Execution

**Inspected · 2026-11-21.** Source and plan baseline `3ceeadfbc`, with a clean tracked tree at entry. SBX-00 through SBX-02 have package evidence and SBX-03 through SBX-05 have partial native integration. This review covers the remaining projection, readiness, execution, and read contracts as they intersect change propagation. It traces original acquisition, release activation and output publication, with annotation and canonical projection as the integration example. The native program source describes the authored behavior. The retained receipts establish their selected test scope, including unchanged repeat execution, and the proposals below have no runtime or performance result. [SBX coordinator](../plans/set-based-execution_2026-11-21.md) · [Earlier review I01 and I02](set-based-execution-after-sbx01_2026-11-21.md#I01) · [Applicable standard](../design_review/design_principles/standard.toml)

## Judgment

Push change information from governed commits. Keep computation driven by current demand, with automatic refresh available for an explicitly maintained, authorized output. Compare stored fingerprints and projection digests at the immediate consumer. Equal inputs preserve its computation. Changed inputs can trigger work, and equal output projections end computational propagation at that branch after settlement. This is the change-locality improvement in H13 and DP-09. It also avoids paying to maintain unused results, a cost that rises as collection-wide edits reach more expensive consumers.

The major integration decision is who establishes freshness and readiness. The current planner's readiness watermark repeatedly walks prerequisites across pinned frontier sweeps. Its observations include supporting episodes, admitted originals, discovery populations and trust. Some role matching can inspect candidate bodies. Push propagation can replace repeated discovery of relevant changes, but computation equality and readiness have independent effects. In a current native test, an unrelated product in the same context leaves a consumer waiting. An authentic second producing episode for the exact same selected product makes that consumer ready. The product's content digest cannot express that change. [Readiness projection](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_operation_projections.py#L2004) · [Body-aware role matching](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_operation_projections.py#L1754) · [Planner wakeup](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_operation_demands.py#L739) · [Exact-pin readiness test](../../python/corpus-structure/tests/semantic/test_data_operation_pinned_reuse_native.py#L538)

| Design | Fit |
|---|---|
| Digest-only checks on demand | Few write-side obligations. Useful for newly requested or rarely read results. Shared deep dependencies can be revisited across separate requests unless settlement is retained and consulted at the right scope. |
| Push change information, execute for demand | Concentrates readiness and freshness work at changes, and retains existing admission and execution ownership. Requires recoverable delivery, reverse selectors that cover previously absent inputs, and an honest read contract while propagation is pending. Best fit for the current plan. |
| Eagerly refresh every reachable output | Can reduce latency for deliberately maintained outputs. A broad release or evidence change can otherwise run unused providers, trigger effect-admission work, and contend with active demand. Use explicit maintenance interest if latency warrants it. |

## I01 · Define change origins and reverse dependency selectors

**Proposed.** Extend SBX-06 with a typed change basis derived from native state transitions. Start an evidence change when the owner makes new evidence current for a maintained selection. Registering immutable bytes under a new document ID is a separate event. For operations, compare effective determinants at release activation. Recipe and relevant policy changes can put a computation in scope, while eligibility or membership changes can require discovery or binding. A new release identifier alone has wider meaning than the computation key. For downstream outputs, use the fields, collections and scope committed at publication. Preserve exact frozen demand selections. An automatically advanced selection needs an explicit live owner or successor demand with its own binding and effect authority. [Original registration](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/original_documents.py#L91) · [Release activation](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/closure_native.py#L561) · [Frozen demand](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_operation_service.py#L36)

Use the effective model to derive reverse lookups over roles, projection selectors and relevant membership scope. A recorded binding supplies the actual selected addresses for existing instances. A declared population or waiting selector supplies the route for additions, removals, first matches and changes to absent inputs. Carry enough identity to distinguish parallel roles, partition scope, placement, and observed order. Query these relations from PostgreSQL. The existing `changed_operations` preview compares old and target recipe and eligibility and walks potential dependencies. Share that definition of change origins where applicable. Its conservative reachability can explain candidates, with actual bindings and digests deciding their consequences. NetworkX can order or explain an explicitly scoped dependency projection using identifiers and edges. Persist the authoritative relationship once and derive any process-local graph from it. Acyclic execution dependencies and the native support-validity fixpoint have different semantics and owners. This preserves DP-05, DP-07 and DP-12 and the graph scope required by CI-05. [Change-impact projection](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/change_impact.py#L157) · [Read projections](../plans/set-based-execution/references-and-projection-keys.md#read-projections) · [Support validity](../adr/0122-set-wise-support-validity.md)

**Acceptance.** Register unrelated evidence and then replace the selected evidence for one scope. Only the latter affects that scope. Test addition and removal of a first eligible member, parallel roles that observe different fields, and operation activation with one material edit and one descriptive edit. The outcomes can be derived independently from the declarations.

## I02 · Settle one dependency step at a time using stored digests

**Proposed.** At the native write boundary, commit each material change with enough durable state to recover its immediate downstream work. Start by examining whether existing publication, release and selection receipts can supply that state. Use the existing executor for claims, admission, retry and settlement. Coalesce repeated work at the same current binding and generation. At a join, wait for the relevant upstream decisions before evaluating the combined key. Fence current advancement against a superseding selection, while retaining the meaning and replay of any legitimately published historical result. Process the affected frontier in bounded sets. A recursive query can help identify a set of candidates when warranted. A walk across every possible descendant would forfeit the output-based stopping condition. This is an execution change under FP-07, H13–H16 and H22–H25, with the atomicity and recovery boundary in DP-19. [Native settlement](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_derivation_native.py#L331) · [Receipt and fulfilled-use settlement](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_derivation_native.py#L653) · [Recovery decision](../adr/0140-durable-recovery-and-exact-stop.md)

SBX-01 already supplies partition content and placement commitments and a field-group digest helper. The current request key still includes full subject context and generic inputs. SBX-06 should compare versioned projection specifications and stored digests before hydration. Use the observed input projection in a consumer's recipe determinant so a change to an unread property leaves that recipe stable. Reuse prepared whole-group roots where repeated whole reads justify them. A changed operation fingerprint may require that operation to execute even when its inputs match. Once it settles, consumers care about their output projections and any other facts their declared contracts observe. An existing demand-resumption test asserts one new receipt for a producer code change and reuse of the original consumer episode for its equal output. Extend that existing reuse decision to the publication-driven path. A computation match still requires current use attachment under the declared substitution policy. Route computation identity through the SBX-06 successor to ADR-0100 and preserve the independent establishment and occurrence rules in ADR-0144 and ADR-0146. Therefore changing an unread lexical field can preserve the canonical computation, while moving a block can change the current assembled canonical projection even when its local geometry stays equal. For a material producer revision, verify that the owner runs and that a byte-equal declared output projection suppresses a new consumer attempt. In a diamond, verify the join settles against both current arms, including when one arm ends propagation unchanged. Exercise a newer edit arriving during execution and replay after the native commit has succeeded but its acknowledgement is lost. ## I03 · Replace repeated readiness walks where the new route covers them

**Proposed.** Retain separate reasons for computational change and for reconsidering a binding. Publish or discover the facts that can wake an exact waiter, including a new usable producing episode, source admission, membership or closure change, applicable proof establishment and changes in trust. Route them to affected selectors with compact metadata and let the current binding owner make the precise admission decision. A conservative wake is acceptable for a body-dependent predicate when the owner checks it once on the affected binding. Use a smaller admitted metadata projection where the existing model can express the predicate faithfully. Keep the digest comparison itself body-free. This applies H5–H7 and H18–H20 to the distinct sources of uncertainty. [Current readiness watermark](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/data_operation_projections.py#L2004) · [Native attachment](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/request_attachment_native.py#L912)

Use the early Revision A chain to identify which existing watermark consumers and frontier-sweep paths can retire. The exact-pin cross-demand test, late discovery, open or closed populations, explicitly selected original admissions and trust changes define the replacement coverage. Preserve bounded execution, refusal, exact resume and the ability to report waiting owners. A transitional uncovered path stays visibly open until its native owner migrates. Retire redundant ancestry expansion and watermark hashing together with those consumers. SBX-06 should own this integration with the executor. SBX-11 supplies keyed observations and SBX-12 preserves pinned and detached evidence. [Executor sweep](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/orchestrate/data_operations.py#L2240) · [Owner-resolved reads](../plans/set-based-execution/owner-resolved-reads.md#sbx-11)

**Acceptance.** Repeat the exact-pin equal-content case alongside first discovery and support revocation. Verify that the independent computational digest stays stable when only readiness changes. Exercise change delivery and recovery at the actual native boundary. Assert that a settled request can be revisited without repeating the replaced recursive scan. Use a small controlled fixture to distinguish the execution paths, with selective native cases for durability and authority.

## I04 · Expose freshness at the same boundary as the selected read

**Proposed.** Let a current-result reader consume a settled change position for its governed scope and report pending, unavailable or unknown when the scope lacks that evidence. Merely checking that the immediate consumer has no queued work is unsafe. An upstream change may have committed while its propagation is still between two edges. Define what covers pending propagation for a read scope, such as a settlement position for an existing maintained selection. A last settled cut remains useful when its meaning is explicit and its authority is established for that read. Avoid a project-wide invalidation generation that makes all unaffected queries reconstruct state. Keep pinned replay tied to captured evidence and continue live authority and revocation checks through their existing native owner. This addresses CI-04 and CI-11 and preserves the owner-resolved read and reuse decisions. [Read decisions](../adr/0147-sbx-owner-resolved-reads.md) · [Continuation and reuse](../adr/0141-serving-continuation-and-reuse.md)

**Acceptance.** Pause a change between publication and a downstream decision. A latest read exposes pending coverage and a historical read retains its cut. After settling an unchanged branch, freshness advances without another provider attempt. Keep an unrelated branch available where its scope is independently resolved. Compare the digest-only acquisition path with explicit body and export requests. The textbook MCP closure timeout is evidence about the cost of a structural body read, with distinct acceptance under SBX-11/12. Smaller freshness reads alone do not resolve that timeout. ## Integration order and limits

Adopt I01 and the identity part of I02 together in SBX-06, after SBX-03–05 supply the relevant addresses, proof establishments and selected binding. Prove the native publication-to-consumer stopping condition in the early annotation chain before broad producer adoption. Establish I03's readiness coverage before deleting the shared watermark path, and coordinate I04 with SBX-11/12. SBX-07 should keep locality conditional on the declared host and cohort determinants. SBX-20–22 can incorporate the two branch-stopping cases and the recovery/read visibility cases into their existing qualification. The analytical kernel trial needs the shared contract only where its actual inputs and locality warrant it. [SBX queue](../plans/set-based-execution_2026-11-21.md#execution-queue) · [Shared change scenarios](../plans/set-based-execution/cutover-and-qualification.md#change-scenarios)

The strongest expected gain is fewer repeated dependency walks and fewer unnecessary downstream attempts where projections stabilize. High-fanout edits and consumers with whole-population or cohort premises can still require broad work. The review recommends demand-driven execution with forward change tracking and identifies explicit maintenance interest as a possible later extension. It proposes reuse of the existing native ownership and recovery structure. Product tests and performance probes were `not_run` for this documentation review. Adoption and any binding decision changes belong to the owning plan and decision routes.
