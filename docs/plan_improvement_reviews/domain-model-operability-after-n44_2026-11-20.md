# Domain Model Operability after N44

**Proposed · 2026-11-20 EST.** Reviewed the remaining DMO-01 through DMO-09 change footprint at
`6a445d6b6`. The coordinator and four companions own adopted work and completion. Older evidence
logs and untracked library notes were already in the checkout. The inspected product and plan paths
matched the baseline. The assessment uses the core 3.3 design principles, efficient-architecture
heuristics, code-intelligence profile 1.4 and test-assessment companion. Read-only investigations
covered producer and recovery paths, consumers and qualification, with an independent challenge of
the proposed boundaries. Coverage follows those paths and their materially affected consumers.

## Judgment

Prioritize one real Standard reuse case with an executable boundary that accounts for dynamic
initialization. A second demand should answer from retained material when only execution transport
changed, with independent native and provider observations of what ran. Bring forward an
interrupted generated-facade trial and a same-cut authority case in parallel. These establish the
mechanisms that downstream migrations will consume. The main additional opportunities are a shared
host grouping rule, one recovery choice per ready item, and treating payload integrity and
validator-family currency as separate obligations throughout migration.

**Tested baseline.** The managed N44 native install and both application admissions report
`passed` (`703ec35909e946cf94441cd844cb24f4`, `99efbb79896545149a94276e841ce20b`,
`711ac13a919d473fabe8fa36ea3db5e6`). The application receipts are on `0ed2825f9`. The later
M1 empty-section case passed and its unchanged repeat created zero receipts. An event run
published both scoring-path receipts before expiring at whole-demand delivery. A scoped rerun reached the numerical
assertion and expired opening support for a second cut. The one-cut replay on `1171491d3` passed
probability, issuer and protected-disclosure checks with 685.91 seconds of fixture setup, 178
seconds of call time and a 1,200-second watchdog. The latter values describe that execution.
These observations establish distinct properties on their recorded sources. Whole-book applicability,
cross-demand Standard reuse and the positive generated-facade transition remain open.

the same invariant at both entry points. Otherwise refresh independently by group and report any
unsupported provider on its actual binding. Keep provider adapters responsible for rebuilding
their own attested artifacts. Use the validated settings owner for effective inputs and redacted
origins. Preserve rechecking changed files or bindings at worker startup.

This is bounded DMO-01/05 work with a DMO-07 consumer. Reuse uv's locked environment check and

## I03 · Make recovery selection explicit at the ready-item boundary

**Refines [N43 I06][n43-i06] and O02.** The executor checks reconciliation, available drafts,
claimed compute and fresh compute across preparation and execution branches. Its important
ordering is visible in the implementation: retained answers can settle before an expensive host
acquisition. Known rollback and acknowledgement loss after an actual commit have separate passed
native controls. Native PDF in-flight cancellation also passed separately. Combining the effects
still requires evidence of original producer retirement. Another coupling appears in
`CompletionDraftStore._open`: it authenticates acknowledgement and body bytes and validates
the retained request using an ambient wire contract. If a supported recovery crosses a schema
transition, that validation can refuse before native applicability is considered.
[Execution branches](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/orchestrate/data_operations.py#L1915) ·
[Draft reader](../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/completion_drafts.py#L191) ·
[Recovery evidence][identity] · [ADR-0140](../adr/0140-durable-recovery-and-exact-stop.md)

**Recommendation.** Let the ready-item owner carry the selected recovery disposition and its
authoritative premise into the executor and operator explanation. Reconcile a known committed
intent, consider a compatible retired producer's private draft or acknowledged pages, and prepare
fresh compute only when inspection permits it. Native settlement continues to own intent,
generation, producer, lease and effect decisions. Keep the inspection fence close to any
new work. Use recorded writer-contract identity when reopening a supported historical draft and
verify current eligibility at adoption. Where a transition cannot safely migrate useful derived
bytes, record regeneration as the disposition under the existing authenticated recovery rules.
Retain smaller branch-local checks if the shared decision cannot express their evidence lifetime.

DMO-02 should supply the recovery disposition, DMO-04 can batch premise acquisition only where
each intent retains its own standing, and DMO-07 can render the same facts. Psycopg transaction
status informs connection state. Current native intent and publication receipts decide what
committed across a lost reply. Challenge the proposed path with an active producer, a replaced
owner after committed acknowledgement loss, and a different validator or writer contract on
reopen. Inspect attempt, lease and publication separately and show that no duplicate provider
work starts. Retire the copied branch logic as those consumers move. This is one owned decision
over existing state transitions and proof owners (FP-03/05/06, H23/H24).

**Recommendation.** Pass the existing authenticated `DemandResult` or equivalent cut handle
through the consumers that need one page, its witnesses and an explanation. Seed support from
selected obligations and preserve any out-of-page determining facts. Preserve explicit proof
paths for distinct original and prediction cuts. Keep current role, epochs and access universes
subject to the native authorization check for new disclosures or effects. PostgreSQL 18
Repeatable Read supplies consistent visibility within its transaction. It supplies no
application authorization. Keep `ProducerRequirement` for shared producer properties and let
specialized issuer, retained receipt and live disclosure checks compose around it. Add another
declared relationship only for a repeated policy with a clear owner.

The smallest useful change is consumer migration to the same-cut path. Try a bounded native
selector for known use IDs if walking frontier pages is still consequential in realistic regional
reads. Compare transaction-local shared work only if retained phases or a focused observation show
repeated computation on the same pins. SQLAlchemy Core connection lifetime and bounded results
can carry the native scope. `yield_per` and result partitions address transfer and buffering
where the route supports them. They leave PostgreSQL server work and prior execution visible for
diagnosis. Retire a second support acquisition or unscoped read for each migrated consumer.
Check late-page selected use, stable equality and order at page boundaries, distinct cuts, and
continuation after a trust or purpose change. For legitimate mixed-family consumption, pair a
public issuer declaring family `none` with protected fit material and reject substituted use
or counterfeit issuer. This keeps semantics and the unit of physical work aligned
(FP-03/04/07, H5/H7/H18/H19, CI-11).

**Refines [N43 I08][n43-i08], related to DMO-08/09.** The N44 event receipting demonstrates
why setup, product execution and inspection need separate attribution. The scoped success
establishes the positive score and disclosure boundary. The actual-provider selection, two

**Recommendation.** Use scoped runs to close distinct properties before assembly. Preserve one
real event journey from producer to selected disclosure, and reuse immutable captured deployment
or setup only with the same source and acquisition premises. Use isolated cheaper cases for
independently authored refusals where provider steps do not affect the property. Keep effective
pytest fixture scope and xdist affinity aligned with the resource owner. Use incremental
reportlog and managed outcomes to distinguish setup, call, teardown, unfinished execution and

prerequisites and first failed phase. For DMO-09, reuse the guarded pre-adoption inventory for

## Integration and evidence limits

I01 has the highest product consequence. Establish the group and resource premises from I02
where the selected workload needs them. I06 is an independent delivery-risk reduction and should
precede the first I05 migration that moves native and facade bytes together. Start I04 with the
event and M1 consumers and I07 wherever an owning operation already supplies a reliable action.
Integrate the I03 recovery disposition before depending on shared completion inspection.
Fold I08's focused evidence into those deliveries and use DMO-08/09 for assembled qualification
and adoption. Retain public D1 equality, native privilege and continuation boundaries, role-specific
issuer checks, and separately recorded project activation.

A general code-analyzer replacement, persistent support cache, separate migration control plane,
or per-operation wire versioning would each need evidence of a workload or rollout that the
bounded alternatives cannot serve. The report records source observations, targeted documentation
research and the standing of retained N43/N44 evidence. The planned discriminating cases above
are future implementation acceptance. This assessment draws on retained execution and source
inspection. Context7 supplied version-matched Docling, PostgreSQL and jsonschema
references. Installed Docling source was used for the specific pipeline observations.
