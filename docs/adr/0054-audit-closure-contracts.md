## Context

The operator accepted review RC01–RC12 on 2026-11-03 and authorized the
[audit-closure series](../plans/audit-closure_2026-11-03.md). Existing declarations,
protected native effects and immutable product readers remain the foundation. This record
qualifies the affected contracts; unrelated clauses of their existing decisions remain in force.
Implementation and qualification are separate obligations owned by that series.

## Options

1. Keep literal classifications, borrowed attribute evidence and broad public reader access.
   Locally smaller changes retain competing semantic authorities and unowned effects.
2. Replace the system with a universal ontology, workflow or provenance service. Additional
   lifecycle and integration responsibilities do not resolve these bounded contract gaps.
3. Extend released products and existing owned operations. Selected: shared prepared semantics,
   protected publication and bounded queries retain existing composition and recovery owners.
   Deterministic walk replay repeats prefix work but avoids a retained traversal service.

## Decision

RC01–03: each source attribute has its own qualified support; generated structural identities
are lineage-bearing assignments. Rule values use an explicit complete versioned ABI.
Storage representation, class display and scientific equivalent-fact selection are separate
released policies. The initial scientific representative is minimum stable eligible fact ID,
with no claim of scientific superiority; original facts and occurrences remain retained.

RC04–07: authored policy validity does not establish executable applicability. Methods receive
owned reads instead of raw connections. One compatible producer binding governs admission,
queue, packets, submission and automation. Configured, reported, manually asserted and verified
execution identity remain distinct. Managed recognition verifies artifacts at startup and binds
every request/response to the admitted service generation, including replacement/restart.
Claims are owned from custody/preparation through durable per-task terminal settlement;
renewal and retirement share synchronization and exact attempt/holder/receipt fences.

RC08–09: ordinary answers expose separate selection, assessment and establishment standing.
Coverage and evidence select before hydration, with bounded references and explicit source
payload reads. Relation queries enumerate directed walks with parallel-edge identity and cycles
through default depth four. New breadth-first ordering and continuations are versioned;
bounded deterministic replay counts all work and refuses unfinished searches without partial
successful answers. I02 settles exact admission/page bounds before activation.

RC10–12: evaluator split effects retain actual immutable policy/seed/population/basis/reason
provenance and exact replay. Unrecoverable history stays explicitly unavailable. Unsupported
reuse choices and unassessed-empty capability claims are removed. Current compiler/compatibility
guidance has one architectural owner. Version changed products and native operations coherently;
frozen history is immutable, supported readers retain their meaning, incompatible use refuses.

Finite rule inference retains the existing atomic publication transaction and complete direct
witnesses while shared indexes and delta processing reduce repeated work. Model/provider and
scientific preparation remain outside publication locks. The change introduces no performance
claim, general scheduler, authentication service or additional assurance framework.

## Consequences

Existing D1–D8 owners carry these accepted targets; the series owns implementation, native
transition, retirement and qualification. AC13 participates in each capability delivery.
Only code displaced in the affected footprint is retired, including newly unused helpers,
tests, fixtures and configuration. Preserve supported readers, frozen migrations, historical
data/grades, plans and evidence. Reopen a target only for a concrete contradiction or a required
capability that cannot meet its premises; missing managed recognition leaves acceptance open.
Complete and qualify AC before revising M1 plans and resuming M1.
