## Context

ADR-0113 establishes exact independently attributed protected A/B/C adjudication and a bounded
ordinary native conclusion, initially for qualified comparisons. Existing condition and definition
publication callers still depend on two old structural authoring functions. Their current reports
already have distinct typed producers and statement meanings. Keeping an old reference store for
those callers would preserve a second publication authority; calling their reports comparisons
would lose domain meaning. The DDO coordinator owns migration, retirement and qualification.

## Options

1. Keep the comparison-only issuer and the old condition/definition authoring path. Rejected:
this retains duplicate selection, history, native APIs and grants after current producers exist.
2. Allow arbitrary same-shaped reports through a generic accepted flag. Rejected: report kind,
actual producing factory and per-policy whole-statement lowering are consequential distinctions.
A controlled final-report fixture cannot stand in for a real producer merely by copying its schema.
3. Extend the existing evaluator through finite typed report choices and policy dispatch. Selected.
It reuses protected original admission, native effect/receipt, trust and authority-leaf custody;
condition/definition integration adds no evaluator registry or second lifecycle.

## Decision

**Accepted target; implementation/qualification Proposed.** One `structure.admit` operation and
`StructuralAdmission` conclusion replace the comparison-specific operation/class, with no alias.
An explicit `report_variant` chooses comparison, condition or definition through three distinct
actual typed report roles. The native guard checks the selected report kind and exact captured
producer/factory/CPU correspondence, not just its JSON shape. All genuine current definition
producer variants remain distinguishable and require their own real inputs.

The protected frame and ordinary conclusion carry an explicit component selector: null for
comparison/condition, required and exact for a definition component. An unrelated selector or a
whole definition report cannot implicitly select one component. Existing per-kind policies and
rubrics own their corresponding source-resolved whole-statement lowering. The same exact original
A/B/C frame/question/rubric/source passage and adjudication-parent rules from ADR-0113 apply.
Only a unique supported C whole statement equal to that faithful lowering can issue a conclusion.
Actor attribution retains its recorded strength; no provider execution, independence, scientific
truth or every-member truth is manufactured.

The existing selected structural request retains explicit report roots and adds explicit
report/admission root pairs. Each paired report must be an explicit report root; one selected cut
acquires both roots and their required support. The reader exposes the bounded ordinary statement
and actual authority leaf while preserving protected details. Existing comparison gap/dossier
policies stay comparison-scoped unless explicitly extended; no whole applicability admission is
implied. Currentness, revocation, missing/unselected output and unavailable support retain their
existing distinctions.

Migrate maintained source/consumer controls through real sentence, construction, predicate and
definition producers with independently specified expected statements. Preserve the distinction
between controlled source decoding, actual provider execution and linguistic correctness. Retire
`eval.create_structural_reference_set(text,text,jsonb)` and
`eval.record_structural_reference_pass(bigint,text,text,bytea,jsonb)` with their final callers,
registration/capture/configuration and mechanism-only fixtures. Preserve useful independent
expectations and unrelated current protected judgment custody.

## Consequences

All three report policies share one native evaluator/publication/reader boundary without sharing
or relabelling their statement meaning. The selected consumer adds explicit same-cut bindings;
there is no implicit newest-reference lookup. The finite dispatch keeps extension local and makes
unsupported policy choices refuse explicitly. No quantitative benefit is claimed.

This extends ADR-0113's initial policy scope; its original adjudication, authority and truthful
attribution rules remain in force. Actual native authoring, source/provider and ordinary selected
read/export/refusal journeys, final old-function retirement and captured successor acceptance stay
with the [DDO coordinator](../plans/declared-data-operations_2026-11-12.md). Acceptance of this
record is not implementation closure or persistent adoption.
