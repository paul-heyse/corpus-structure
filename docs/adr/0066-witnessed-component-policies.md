## Context

The audited production-enrichment review supplies RC01–RC06. The operator accepted them for
planning on November 5 and authorized WCE00–03 execution on November 6. The operator then
replaced RC05's enterprise packet with statistics qualification and domain-neutral design.
A useful located component must survive an unresolved stronger interpretation.

## Options

1. Retain only whole structural reports. Rejected: this discards supported parts and hides
   whether location, role, attachment or applicability failed.
2. Introduce a separate witness store and workflow. Rejected: existing immutable contributions,
   structural reports, protected references and common publication already own these lifecycles.
3. Extend those contracts with versioned components and exact operation questions. Selected:
   this preserves existing custody and batches while separating meanings and authority.

## Decision

RC01 introduces additive witnessed components with named endpoints, source occurrence, declared
basis, scope, dependencies and explicit interpretation outcomes. Retention, independent assessment
and public standing are separate immutable relations. An independently supported component can
qualify only its exact statement through an operation-specific policy; it cannot qualify its
containing report or establish scientific truth. ADR-0065's whole-report policies remain in force.

RC02 permits question-relative compatible relations and exclusive alternative groups. Denotation
exclusions retain their identity meaning and do not forbid comparison. RC03 accepts typed,
versioned comparison beside lexical comparison. RC04 accepts supported-facet dossier display
selection independently of membership qualification. Their algorithms remain later WCE scope.

RC06 freezes source opportunities before alignment/filtering, records provenance groups and
exposure partitions, and distinguishes source-first capture from output-first correctness.
Protected source authoring and exact A/B/C parent custody remain required. Historical frames
retain their original version and development-only meaning.

RC05 is replaced by the November 6 operator direction: qualify on the statistics set; keep
vocabulary, predicates, role bindings and policies domain-owned. Enterprise scenarios exercise
generic contracts but require no enterprise corpus acquisition or efficacy campaign. A different
identity event is not established merely by qualifying statistics extraction.

Prepare matchers and source closure once per useful scope, keep CPU/provider work outside
publication transactions, and batch components through existing native lifecycle owners.
No new generic graph service, task per primitive or independent reference registry is selected.

## Consequences

WCE00 owns adoption and verification-scope reconciliation; WCE01/02 own shared contracts and
assessment; WCE03 owns named annotation candidates. Later packages retain their own implementation
and qualification obligations. This accepted decision establishes the target, not implementation
or measured benefit. No default activation follows. Existing outputs, readers, migrations and
evidence keep their original meaning.
