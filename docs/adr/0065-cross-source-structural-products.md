## Context

PCE-10 needs definitions, qualified comparisons and dossiers spanning existing source projects.
The source-contribution contract belongs to one original source-view task. Its project guards
remain meaningful. Curator descriptors also cannot be relabelled as source quotations.

## Options

1. Copy sources into a merged project or relax source-view ownership. Rejected: this obscures
   original custody and makes local source operations depend on cross-project policy.
2. Store cross-document results in a separate semantic store and scheduler. Rejected: this
   duplicates immutable report, task, publication, recovery and assessment responsibilities.
3. Add finite RUN-unit structural reports with explicit original source references. Selected:
   the existing lifecycle remains the owner, while group inputs make cross-source meaning explicit.

## Decision

A structural report declares its original projects, completed runs/cuts, releases/result revisions,
source views, native products, projection/coordinate recipes and exact cited passages. Native task
completion validates those references under their original ownership. Definition applicability,
qualified comparison and dossier membership are separate finite report variants. They start as
private proposals; resemblance, lexical equality and report validation grant no public authority.

Group assessments retain the complete source frame, not an arbitrary first-source surrogate.
Public eligibility must use operation-specific witnesses for the exact structural statement.
Dossier membership and representative display remain distinct. Existing stronger record and
scientific-truth boundaries remain in force.

A structural product identity binds its typed envelope and candidate-list reference, excluding
the numeric retrieval report. The immutable task result and its result digest retain that full
derivation. This avoids Python/PostgreSQL numeric-spelling differences changing product custody.
Public claim identity is narrower again: it binds the source-resolved semantic statement and its
qualifications, while execution identifiers and representative display remain provenance.

Admitted structural statements use a supplemental row within the existing PublicationBatch and
realization. This follows the existing local-semantic assertion route rather than inventing graph
facts or a separate publisher. A native operation-specific policy requires a supported whole
public statement under exact current independent group C/A/B custody, original source closure
and actual upstream product dependencies. Supplied member qualifications alone grant no authority.

Review exports share captured closure and atomic no-replace publication. Ordinary selections do
not elevate reader authority. Authorized feedback delivery records exposure before labels leave
the protected reader, preserving original partitions and passes.

## Consequences

Original projects stay isolated; bounded group reads validate each distinct source closure once.
Readers settle before CPU/model work, with inputs rechecked before immutable publication. This
adds finite contracts and native validation but avoids copying books or introducing another store.

The decision is accepted; production integration, assessment, public eligibility and assembled
qualification remain separately tracked by the [PCE coordinator](../plans/progressive-corpus-enrichment_2026-11-04.md).
No default activation follows.
