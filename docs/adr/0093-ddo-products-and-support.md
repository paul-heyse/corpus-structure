## Context

The target must reuse equal semantic products without inventing independent evidence or losing original source identity. Task/run origin alone cannot define product meaning or support validity.

## Options

1. Keep task-qualified products and copied reused tasks. This confuses value with authentic execution.
2. Merge equal bytes and discard episodes. This loses custody and independent evidence.
3. Separate semantic products, exact blobs, authentic derivations and selected support. Selected.

## Decision

A semantic product is kind contract plus immutable subject/context plus canonical body. Original source occurrences, role/order/multiplicity and local member qualification remain exact. Derivations retain actual execution, runtime and consumed support. Equal bytes do not establish source equivalence, trust or independent votes.

Native admission validates semantic request and full selected admission manifests separately. Cross-release inputs require compatible contracts, source/custody/coverage/outcome/purpose and current support. Value substitution is explicitly admitted and does not rewrite history or fabricate inference. Directly revoked producing execution cannot be rehabilitated by replacing only upstream support. Trust publication/revocation shares ordered scoped epoch authority; large preparation stays outside locks. Native permissions and exact requested/observed numerical evidence are preserved.

## Consequences

This accepts the target, not completed implementation or native adoption. The
[DDO coordinator](../plans/declared-data-operations_2026-11-12.md) owns package readiness and original
finding dispositions. Current selected protocols and archived interpretations keep their existing
ABI/custody guards until the coordinated adoption package replaces their callers. These records
establish the DDO target; full-record retirement of ADR0063/0060/0027/0081/0082/0084/0090/0033/0014/
0085/0091/0050/0064/0067 is not performed while those records still govern live legacy consumers.
DDO-00 records the scoped replacement route; DDO-11 completes reference retirement at cutover.
No data deletion, migration, activation or provider call is authorized by this record alone.
