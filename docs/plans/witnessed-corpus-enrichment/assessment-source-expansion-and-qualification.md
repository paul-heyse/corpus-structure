## WCE02 — questions, sources and frozen references

### Early contract slice and independent authoring

The early WCE02 slice supplies source questions, rubrics and source-first frames before new
production. Output-first frames/grades are frozen after their respective producers execute;
they do not delay unrelated producer design or count as available labels before they exist.
After WCE01 defines each semantic question, extend frames with operation/version, exact source and
product selection, source/output direction, rubric/context version, source group, exposure partition,
question-specific opportunities and explicit inspected scope. Freeze original source opportunities
before token alignment, candidate generation, score filtering or publication. Output-first frames
reference exact emitted IDs; source-first cases can exist without any prediction or candidate ID.
Candidate/grammar constraints cannot delete unrepresentable source spans from the denominator.

Use development, feature-fit, calibration/threshold-selection and final group-held-out roles with
explicit exposure history. One physical source family/context defines leakage grouping even if
many windows, crops or producers refer to it. Group assignments are authored from provenance, not
inferred by a library splitter. Freeze final-source groups before tuning. If a final cohort is exposed,
relabel its role and author new held-out cases rather than continuing an unseen-origin claim.

Questions include term/defining-content discovery, semantic role, endpoint attachment, usage sense,
applicability, unit/note scope, symbol declaration, comparator/interval delta, dossier membership and
supported-facet coverage. Record supported/unsupported/ambiguous/unassessed and source-state/coverage
separately. An uninspected header is not an unstated header; missing DEP is not absent definition.
Partial NER outside/missing/blocked annotations belong to their training component, not universal truth.

Independent source authors see the original selected source/rubric, not producer predictions or
protected expected labels from another partition. Retain source-only A/B and adjudication C where
the existing protocol requires them. Disagreements retain their exact question, evidence and reason;
there is no majority-vote promotion to source truth. Existing literal analytic examples can challenge
mechanisms, but synthetic fixtures alone cannot establish corpus efficacy or enterprise transfer.

## New evaluation and benefit contracts

| Claim | Independent observation and reporting |
|---|---|
| Quantity | Newly supported distinct physical occurrences, fields and typed relations over the same declared source opportunities; repeated windows are not independent facts. |
| Quality | Location, role, endpoint identity, direction, attachment, condition, scope, applicability and abstention separately; correct span earns no automatic role credit. |
| Connectivity | Independently identified correct cross-item/source edge/path opportunities and false bridges; graph density alone is not success. |
| Synthesis | New reproducible source-supported result requiring multiple qualified premises; record missing premises and weaker supported conclusions. |
| Capture versus interpretation | Source/annotation availability, token-unrepresentability, candidate discovery, inventory, retrieval, classification, constraints and admission are different outcomes. |
| Scope | Eligible population, examined/inspected subset, omitted material, group/exposure status and bounded continuation are explicit. |

For each composition compare A, B and A+B at fixed endpoints first, then allow discovery. Retain a
case ledger of newly correct, corrected, lost, spurious and still-undecidable cases under identical
questions; outcome comparisons cannot silently change the population. Preserve zero-output and failed
operations. Determine reference coverage from source inventory, not from surviving predictions.

### Library realization

Use existing protected frame/grade services and pure operation scoring. sklearn1.9.1
`cross_validate(return_estimator=True, return_indices=True)` supports native per-fold fitted models
and held-out indices; `PrecisionRecallDisplay.from_cv_results` can render them. Underlying tables,
fold predictions, sample/group/weight provenance and failures remain the comparison artifact.
`DummyClassifier` supplies prior/constant baselines. GroupKFold/StratifiedGroupKFold preserve supplied
groups but cannot establish statistical independence. Fit preprocessing inside folds and separate
fit/calibration/threshold/final populations. FrozenEstimator preserves fitted state, not independence.
Source: [sklearn addendum §5.5/§6](../../libraries/scientific_compute/Scikit-learn.md),
[tagged validation](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn/blob/1.9.1/doc/modules/cross_validation.rst),
[tagged metrics change](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn/blob/1.9.1/doc/whats_new/v1.9.rst).
Mechanisms are Interface-checked; new event frames, reference data and outcomes remain Proposed.

Use Splink's relational error/evaluation tables for its scoped pilot, with clerical-truth and predicted
match thresholds explicitly distinguished. Evaluate independent eligible pairs and blocking omissions
outside scored-pair-only diagnostics. Weighted/purposive defect samples do not become probability
samples for PPI; group counts/facets do not manufacture independent layout variation for Pyro/GEE.
Those conditional methods retain their own estimand/model prerequisites in the mathematical companion.

union from the actually implemented packages and recorded conditional decisions. Any unsatisfied
required prerequisite remains open. Do not declare a provider route passed from a mock, an unsupported

its mechanism, or record that the simpler baseline suffices for the selected question. Distinguish
implementation completion from unestablished broad quality. No calibrated truth or full-corpus claim
follows. Performance claims require measurements; qualitative execution fit alone makes no speedup claim.
