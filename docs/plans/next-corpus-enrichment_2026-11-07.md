## Outcome and inspected foundation

Recover useful structure from material already in the corpus and carry it through to a
reader with its precise evidence and standing. Start with exact captions, qualified
predicate frames and explicit links between occurrences. Use those products to compare
statements, inspect dossier membership and explain missing links. Table correspondence and
attachment-event fitting offer parallel development comparisons with their own inputs and
evidence. A weaker or unavailable statistical result leaves the structural products usable.

## Shared design decisions

- **Source and semantic authority.** The original native product establishes physical
  occurrence and coordinate space. A selected source view establishes projected text and
  membership. Scope, host, exact endpoint roles, and original evidence identify a proposed
  attachment. A parser, model, similarity score, or fit supplies a versioned observation or
  policy result. Independent support and admitted knowledge have their own contracts.
  Preserve unresolved alternatives and explicitly distinguish unavailable work, invalid input,
  no candidate, no supported selection, and negative evidence.
- **Released contracts and adoption.** Use existing LinkML, native PostgreSQL validation,
  prepared method/operation contracts, and protected assessment. Coordinate changed semantic
  types, native admission, ordinary reads, MCP, CLI and offline export verification within each
  affected package. Preserve authentic originals and immutable receipts. Convert derived
  products only where their premises establish the new meaning, and otherwise regenerate under
  the new version. Keep effects explicit and version activation separate from diagnostics and
  candidate comparison. [Architecture decisions](../design/DESIGN.md#section-2) ·
  [ADR-0063](../adr/0063-semantic-runtime-identity.md) ·
  [ADR-0065](../adr/0065-cross-source-structural-products.md) ·
  [ADR-0067](../adr/0067-witnessed-current-only-cutover.md)
- **Execution fit.** Prepare source membership and reusable rule assets once per valid
  selection. Reuse authenticated bytes and annotations when their dependencies match. Scope
  candidates before fuzzy scoring, prefer setwise SQL for ordinary reads, and keep source
  mapping, provider work and numerical fitting outside long database transactions. Make
  examined work visible in the design when a small answer could require a large scan, graph
  expansion or factor intermediate. A native scorer or alternate executor must earn its
  additional preparation and failure surfaces through a concrete workload. Protect atomic
  publication and cancellation at the existing owner boundaries. [Design standard](../design_review/design_principles/standard.toml) ·
  [Execution heuristics](../design_review/design_principles/core/efficient-architecture-heuristics.md)
- **Decision boundary.** The selected targets fit the current source, scope, release, assessment
  and publication authorities. Broader source membership, a new semantic authority or an
  expanded publication policy requires its own justified decision if an investigation reaches
  that boundary. Route any change to a binding rule through its governing design and operator
  decision before dependent work enters scope. Follow the binding’s targeted review cadence
  for substantial stages and assembled changes. The foundation assessment here is scoped
  to the proposed extensions. [Review cadence](../design_review/design_principles/binding/corpus-structure.md#reviews-in-this-repository)

## Qualification and handoff

- **Local implementation checks.** Each package supplies exact positive and refusal or
  uncertainty examples, expected native outcome, and affected consumer journey. Start with
  focused `uv run ci test -- PATH` and applicable compile or provider checks. Use an
  independently specified outcome for semantic judgments and arithmetic where reusing the
  implementation would mask the defect.
- **Joined behavior.** At completed selected functional scope, verify the ordinary
  source-to-reader path with exact native source/cut pins, changed release contracts,
  repeatability, protected custody and cancellation where relevant. Use actual provider
  results for provider claims. Cover the current Reader, CLI, MCP and detached replay paths
  for the affected products, including refusal, missing prerequisites, and pagination. Run
  applicable `uv run ci test` and `uv run ci check` on final source and context. Account for
  the required union, including any native fault controls needed by the changed lifecycle.
  A coincident stage and increment end shares one design/target review.
- **Benefit comparison.** Freeze opportunities and independent judgments before inspecting
  output. Compare common candidates to isolate selection, scoring or assembly. Run actual
  discovery separately to find omissions and additional supported structure. Account for
  the three native-only captions, formula `Z` with the false affine-`X` control, grouped
  qualifiers and embedded negation, rooted comparison sides, distinct gap statuses, matched
  table roles and exact attachment labels. Record unresolved, unavailable, corrected, lost
  and spurious results explicitly. Development results describe the shared frame. Additional
  transfer, calibration or performance claims need their own evidence.
- **Current checkpoint.** The series is proposed. Runtime, provider, fitting and adoption
  checks for its packages are `not_run` at authoring. The first proposed execution work is
  NCE-01 caption emission and NCE-02's neutral frame/qualification boundary. Any unfulfilled
  conditional package remains visible at its owner, with the missing premise or a justified
  disposition.
