## Implement the shared correspondence question

1. **Prepare exact premises.** Start with the observed Table 5.2 counts and unlabeled margins,
   homologous schematic Table 5.3 and its explanatory prose in the existing retained frame.
   Construct candidates from eligible exact cell and schematic occurrences, their row/column
   geometry, and the declaration that relates their meanings. Keep stable identities for the
   source revision, selection, evidence and correspondence alternatives. Where the source
   establishes a correspondence, retain it as a fixed premise. Represent genuine ambiguity as
   a finite auxiliary decision `M` with a distinct unresolved state. A single unresolved state
   covers unresolved readings within the retained universe. Omitted mass stays unknown.
2. **Define the registered policy.** Give the new policy its own identity and version. Keep each
   existing cell-role choice `R_j` independently unresolved. Enter the shared evidence once
   as a unary log potential on `M`, and add local `(M, R_j)` compatibility factors with exact
   dependencies. Where `M` is fixed, reduce those factors against the fixed premise. Allow
   compatible notes, units and nested roles to coexist. Use hard masks for established
   incompatibilities such as disjoint exact columns. Preserve fallible geometry or linguistic
   preferences as soft evidence with explicit weights. A local exception can change one role's
   compatibility while leaving other roles under the same correspondence.
3. **Make the new request reproducible at admission.** Extend the existing registered policy
   branch and its request/result custody in the
   [native contribution admission](../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/sql/source_contributions.sql).
   Resolve exact admitted source anchors, geometry, schematic correspondence evidence and
   scoped declaration. Bind them and the policy version to a canonical premise/request
   projection. Establish one authoritative construction of the ordered domain, factors, masks
   and event definitions from those premises, shared by request preparation and trusted
   validation. Prefer deriving the bounded canonical request at the existing native preparation
   or admission boundary and returning the identical request to the worker, so the worker
   does not author a second rule implementation. If that boundary needs a shared trusted
   derivation route, make native admission compare against its independently bound request
   identity and authoritative content. A producer-authored hash alone cannot authenticate a
   changed factor. Keep inference out of the admission transaction. Bind and validate producer,
   executor, policy, request and result identities through the existing operation contracts.
4. **Deliver through the existing routes.** Expose the correspondence premise, local factors,
   coherent alternatives and unresolved or unavailable reasons to Reader, CLI, MCP and detached
   export through the existing interpretation contract. Keep independently admitted table
   components usable on invalid, infeasible, numerical, cancelled or over-budget outcomes.
   Publish the new policy as an explicit release selection. Regenerate only affected
   interpretations and dependent assembly products through the existing operation lineage.
   Archived cuts keep their original policy and custody. Interrupted regeneration or a failed
   admission leaves the last completed cut available.
