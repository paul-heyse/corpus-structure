## Result and boundaries

PCE-10 delivers three related capabilities: retrieve a definition or explanation applicable to
an anchored usage; nominate and compare qualified clauses or workflow steps; and assemble cited
concept/provisional-sense dossiers. Each is useful before every source record is complete.
Their shared value is preservation of source distinctions across documents: relevant passages
stay distinct from supported relationships, and source statements stay distinct from scientific
truth or an operator's choice of governing policy.

The target is a composition of existing operation, source-reader, relationship and qualification
contracts. D1/D2 own product meanings; D4/D5 own interpretation and scoped relationship semantics;
D8 owns the serving/query boundary. PCE-01/02 provide typed contributions and adapters; PCE-03
provides operation-specific assessment; PCE-04 provides qualified local public admission;
PCE-05/06 provide contextual retrieval and scoped selection; PCE-07/08 provide parsing and
mathematical interpretation where required; PCE-09 provides complete review/export; PCE-11
owns final union accounting. PCE-10 owns its producers, query composition and migrated consumers,
not a second semantic store, workflow engine or assessment ledger.

## Baseline and foundation assessment

The current [source-search owner](../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/serve/source_search.py)
returns pinned source selections, excerpts, lexical scores and bounded continuations without
requiring completed knowledge. It is useful for passage discovery; an excerpt match does not
establish that a passage defines a usage. Current
[generic queries](../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/serve/queries.py),
[semantic read sessions](../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/query.py)
and [source/explanation boundary](../../design/D8-agent-and-query-interfaces.md#source-product-query-and-explanation-boundary)
already carry selected release/result scope, occurrence/source evidence and explicit coverage.
The [companion export owner](../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/serve/companion_export.py)
supplies complete captured-source export and comparison machinery, not a dossier semantics owner.

These foundations favor adding typed named operations and projections, then composing them
through the same read boundary. A generic semantic search response or generated prose blob
would force downstream consumers to guess applicability, identity and coverage. Conversely,
requiring a complete accepted record for every useful definition or clause would repeat the
all-or-nothing bottleneck identified in O01/O09. The selected route retains independently
qualified local products with explicit dependencies and stronger composite admission when the
consumer needs it. It adds the semantic distinctions needed by these real consumers without
introducing a general document-comparison framework.

## Shared product contract

Every result binds its request/question, selected source and semantic release/result context,
input contribution identities, relationship/qualification policy and coverage. Source addresses
retain their actual precision and producing task/product identities. Equal text in two sources
remains two occurrences. Proposed, accepted, ambiguous, rejected and unsupported contributions
remain visible under explicit inclusion policy; one successful primitive does not qualify an
assembled conclusion.

A structural result carries independently addressable claims/memberships and evidence references.
Display summaries derive from those products. Each claim identifies the source statement or
interpretation it reports, its scope/version, qualification and supporting or conflicting
occurrences. A statement that a source calls a result a theorem does not prove that theorem.
A statement that two policies differ does not select which policy applies to an organization.

Coverage distinguishes source selection, recognized inputs, candidate retrieval and assessed
relationships. An empty bounded result means no result under those inputs and policies; it does
not establish no definition, no exception or no other sense. Truncation and incomplete recognition
must reach the public response and exported product. Failed/cancelled prerequisites are explicit
outcomes rather than omitted members in an apparently complete dossier.

## PCE-10 — Implementation slices and prerequisites

The package has three usable slices with different prerequisite grains. Define their operation
and read contracts with PCE-01 early. Actual definition retrieval needs implemented PCE-02
anchored usages/definition targets, PCE-04 local eligibility/admission, PCE-05 lists, PCE-06
applicable relationship decisions and PCE-03 references. The PCE-04 prerequisite applies to
every public slice, not only dossiers. Clause comparison additionally needs qualified role/condition/version
contributions from PCE-01/02 and, where required by the selected question, PCE-07/08. Dossiers
need qualified local serving from PCE-04 and completed membership/traceability semantics from
the first two slices. Complete portable export adoption composes with PCE-09; it is not a reason
to defer the working reader of each slice.

### Slice 1: definition and explanation retrieval

Given a pinned occurrence, explicit question and eligible definition/explanation inventory,
generate a PCE-05 shortlist and ask PCE-06 whether each passage defines/explains the usage in
its context. Retain the target passage's own scope and provenance, including its parent heading
and any limitations. A definition target can be a source-backed passage or provisional sense;
it need not have a global canonical entity.

The response distinguishes applicable definition, applicable explanation, competing plausible
readings, relevant mention/example without definition, inventory gap, retrieval miss established
by assessment, and insufficient source support. An ordinary production response cannot claim a
retrieval miss merely because no target was selected; that diagnosis requires known membership
or independent assessment. Explicit definition language is useful evidence, not a rule that every
definition must use a fixed marker. A paragraph giving examples of normal distributions does not
automatically define "normal" in a usage concerning standard normal variables.

Integrate the typed result into the ordinary named query surface and bounded explanation reader.
Expose passage and usage source selections so a reader can inspect both without reconstructing
anchors from snippets. PCE-09 exports the declared-scope inventory, rejected/ambiguous candidates,
selected support and coverage. Preserve the candidate-list identity for assessment and replay.

### Slice 2: qualified clause and workflow comparison

Nomination asks which clauses/steps merit comparison; assessment asks what they share and where
they differ. Candidate similarity provides nomination only. Each clause/step retains actor,
action, object, obligation/modality, condition, threshold/unit, exception, order/dependency,
parent procedure and applicable version where observed. Unknown fields remain unknown.
The comparison binds both original occurrences and their independently qualified components;
unsupported field inference cannot manufacture equivalence.

For example, "must approve above $25,000" and "may review above $50,000" may be useful neighbors.
The product must preserve the action, modality and threshold differences. Do not classify them
as duplicates, aliases or equivalent policies from shared wording. An onboarding process,
activation step, checklist and system action may participate in one workflow while remaining
different kinds. Order comparison requires established order/dependency evidence; adjacency,
page position or a shared heading alone does not establish a process dependency.

Start with source-supported field comparisons and domain-owned compatibility rules. Return
qualified shared aspects, differences, incompatible applicability, alternative interpretation or
insufficient evidence. A declared equivalence/duplicate operation, if needed by the selected
consumer, must state its required dimensions and refuse a positive result when an unknown
meaning-bearing dimension prevents it. Comparison links remain separate from identity and never
override exclusions. Numerical or mathematical equivalence uses PCE-08's governed interpretation
with its conditions/exactness limits rather than a new string-based scientific checker.

Expose a pair comparison with citations per dimension and a bounded candidate-pair inventory.
Filter eligible corpus/scope/version groups and nominate top candidates before hydrating full
evidence; avoid exhaustive all-pairs comparison or a request per field. A request to compare
across versions preserves both versions and reports their applicability. It does not silently
normalize one away or choose a current version.

### Slice 3: concept and provisional-sense dossiers

Build a dossier as a cited structural assembly rooted in a selected concept or provisional
sense under explicit corpus, scope and version selections. Include source-backed definitions
and scope; accepted and proposed terminology; representative usages; typed relationships;
variants; exceptions; conflicting interpretations; unresolved senses and open questions. Retain
original occurrences, per-member qualifications and contribution identities. The dossier may
contain both accepted and proposed members when the response states their standing.

Membership follows selected typed relationships and declared compatibility, not graph proximity
or a nearest-neighbor cluster. A dossier can group several unresolved senses while displaying
their separation; it cannot collapse them into canonical identity. Conflict is a retained product,
not an instruction to pick one interpretation. A missing required subsection reports its source,
inventory or assessment coverage limit instead of filling prose from model knowledge.

Choose representative excerpts after grouping by sense, role, scope and effective version.
Preserve every designated exception/revision/conflicting reading in the complete membership
product; representative display does not erase it. Use a bounded deterministic representative
policy first. If repetitive wording overwhelms useful examples, MMR-style selection may trade
query relevance against redundancy within those groups, with stable ties and a recorded policy.
It must not remove required exceptions, choose truth or substitute for membership assessment.
The [MMR paper](https://aclanthology.org/X98-1025/) supplies the diversity mechanism; its results
do not qualify this dossier implementation. The
[retrieve-then-assess pattern](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.519/) supports separating
nomination from relationship decisions, without importing its benchmark as corpus evidence.

Store/reuse the structural membership and support references through the existing immutable
operation/report lifecycle. Render a summary from those references. If prose synthesis is used,
every factual sentence must map to a cited member and its qualifications; unsupported synthesis
is omitted or returned as a failed/unresolved synthesis outcome. Source-grounded dossier claims
are claims about what selected sources state and how supported contributions relate, not an
adjudication of scientific truth. No new language-model generation dependency is required merely
to display the structured dossier.

## Integration, migration and execution fit

Implement shared declarations and query/report shapes through PCE-01's released model and
current compiler; update D1/D2/D4/D5/D8 owners through the coordinator's accepted RC routes.
Native storage/publication changes use existing forward migration/admission owners. Preserve
archived releases, original source selections, raw reports, failed attempts and old complete-record
grades. New definition/comparison/dossier references are new products; do not reinterpret old
record labels or regenerate historical bytes to make them fit.

Migrate the actual ordinary query/CLI/registered MCP consumers for the selected capability,
explanation reader and complete review/export path. Keep expensive model/parser imports out of
ordinary registration and retained reads. Version public results and continuations when their
meaning changes, pinning source/product membership and policy. Unsupported old/new combinations
refuse through the established compatible-reader/rebuild route; avoid speculative compatibility
layers. When a shared contract changes, producer, reader, assessor and export consumers move in
the same coherent slice. PCE-09 owns its export implementation, while PCE-10 supplies the semantic
membership and completeness requirements it must retain.

Perform eligibility and narrow projection before source hydration. Reuse pinned target vectors,
source closure and selected relationship products where their determinants remain valid.
Adding a source definition can change retrieval or dossier membership without changing an
unchanged usage vector. Changed scope/version policy or identity exclusion may invalidate a
relationship and downstream dossier even when wording is unchanged. Counterevidence, absent
lookups and inventory membership participate in computational dependencies where consequential.
Do not repeat full protected source-custody reconstruction per dimension, member or excerpt.

Resource ownership follows existing reader/task/publication boundaries. Borrow snapshot access
for selected reads, release it before encoder or comparison CPU work, and recheck intended pins
before immutable publication. Bound candidate pairs, hydrated excerpts, output rows and bytes
independently; output limits alone do not bound examined work. Complete dossier/export membership
streams through ordered pages rather than loading every source payload in memory. A partial
display carries an explicit continuation/completeness state and does not become a complete export.

Cancellation stops new pair/synthesis work, cancels or drains actual owned child/iterator work,
closes source readers/sinks and settles snapshots before propagating. Export staging publishes
the destination only after successful settlement through the existing no-replace route. Retained
completed upstream products remain reusable after a later comparison/render failure. Preserve
the primary failure while attempting independent cleanup; avoid retries at several layers.
No durable per-dossier scheduler, duplicate reference store or global cache is warranted by this
single-operator workflow. The benefit hypothesis is useful bounded structural access before
whole-record completion; no quantitative throughput or effectiveness claim is made.

## Acceptance, population scope and remaining choices

Use a frozen statistics slice first, with independently authored definition applicability,
relationship, difference and dossier membership judgments under PCE-03. The slice includes
normal/standard-normal senses, parent-scoped parameter names, definition-versus-example and
version/scope ambiguity. Enterprise clauses and workflow examples are illustrative transfer
cases. When actual representative documents become available, freeze a bounded source slice
and author independent judgments before assessing transfer. This is neither a required second
released corpus nor grounds for an enterprise performance claim. Missing material is reported
as an unavailable transfer prerequisite at the coordinator, not disguised with synthetic quality
results; targeted synthetic cases may still establish a narrow semantic control.

Independent evidence must expose each stage's wrong answer:

- Give reference usages and a known inventory to retrieval; distinguish inventory coverage from
  correct-target inclusion/rank. Give fixed lists to definition/relationship assessment, including
  plausible mentions/examples and the wrong sense/version. Scorer/model improvements cannot
  mask an upstream omission by changing the reference population.
- Compare clauses with independent expected actor/action/modality/threshold/exception/version
  differences, unknown dimensions and distinct workflow roles. Include apparently equivalent
  wording whose parent scope differs, and a comparison edge with an identity exclusion.
- Assess dossier membership, missing senses, exceptions/revisions/conflicts, source support,
  duplication and traceability directly. A known complete membership set challenges a renderer
  that omits inconvenient material; a cited passage that merely mentions the topic must not
  establish a synthesized definition claim.
- Exercise source/result pinning across query, explanation and complete export; changing inventory,
  scope policy or a member's qualification invalidates affected reuse/continuations. Check bounded
  pages, cancellation and staged-output refusal without re-running unchanged upstream extraction.
- Use actual selected encoder/provider results where the claim depends on them; separate a
  retained structural replay or mocked adapter control from native provider qualification.

PCE-03 owns assessment frames and thresholds; PCE-11 owns assembled same-source/context union
accounting. No agent task benchmark is required. Preserve existing full-record evidence and its
stronger meanings. Package-local controls do not establish a complete corpus, scientific validity,
external host attachment or operational adoption. Actual commands and dated outcomes are recorded
at the coordinator/evidence owner when execution occurs, not invented here.

Before executing each slice, choose the frozen inventory/population and the exact relation or
comparison question with PCE-03; define mandatory dimensions for any positive equivalence claim;
and select the version/scope grouping and required exception membership policy. These are
bounded semantic decisions under PCE-01/06, not reasons to start a general ontology or solver
framework. Optional reranking, MMR and generated prose require evidence of the stated problem
and their own qualified configuration; initial structured delivery does not depend on them.
