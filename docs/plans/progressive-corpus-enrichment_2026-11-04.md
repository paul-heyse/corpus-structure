## Outcome and authority

Progressively enrich the corpus with source-grounded, independently assessable structure. Each
operation declares the distinction or relationship it adds, preserves its evidence and exposes
what it captured, missed, misidentified or linked incorrectly. Retain useful contributions even
when a later operation cannot assemble a complete record. Complete structured knowledge remains
the direction; complete-record acceptance is a stronger claim rather than the universal progress gate.

The input is the [progressive enrichment review](../functional_reviews/progressive-corpus-enrichment_2026-11-04.md)
at publication `a3b0f21d`, O01–O10 and RC01–RC08, and the unchanged operator-supplied
[revised target](../target-pipeline-design/production-miletone-one-revised.md), SHA-256
`3ed1168d594e6a69e63dca3fdc2345bfea9a3847e7715f35163c53286142177e`.
Inspected baseline: `a3b0f21d`, 2026-11-04. Subsequent implementation must reconcile intervening
changes before consuming this baseline. DESIGN/D1–D8 and accepted ADRs remain architectural
authority until successor decisions implement the accepted changes. Apply core 3.3, efficient
architecture heuristics 1.0 and relevant code-intelligence 1.4 guidance through `standard.toml`.
Plan authoring does not deploy schema, install dependencies, restart training, change defaults or
establish any new product qualification.

English statistics is the continuity corpus. Enterprise glossary/procedure examples challenge
generality; they are not another released corpus. CPU extraction, attributable interventions,
source fidelity, scoped semantic authority and protected publication survive. Source reading is
not scientific truth, causal inference or theorem verification. Agent-task benchmarks are not
release criteria. No full-book reread or producer rerun is a prerequisite to inspecting retained output.

## Baseline and foundation assessment

**Implemented; source-inspected 2026-11-04, qualification bounded by original receipts.**
`ObservationDraft` and released source-observation declarations already retain typed/versioned
payloads, coordinates and input references separately from admitted knowledge. Native reports,
effective profiles and preparation identity survive assembly failure. Current diagnostic score
projection can omit unsupported confidence shapes; a new contribution adapter must consume the
returned objects, not infer that absent projected scores mean absent spans.

The public record projection still roots structure in assembled records. The protected evaluator
aligns record occurrences before core/full/evidence grading. Whole-reading fitting labels and
calibration do not supply local correctness. The bounded interpreter uses log potentials but
enumerates retained Cartesian support; dependence components are diagnostic, not elimination.
Reusable linguistic columns, native table/formula addresses, task fencing, source custody and
captured readers are suitable foundations, with changes at their existing owners rather than a
second observation store, workflow engine or solver registry.

The [retained-case comparison](../design_review/evidence/2026-11-04_progressive-enrichment-review/README.md)
found 80 occurrence-matched table candidates agreeing on cell/header/value/caption semantics and
evidence, while row-label/unit/note differed. That subset is evidence for recovering useful
contributions, not local population accuracy or a revised protected grade. Historical zero accepted
records and failed fits retain their exact contract and meaning. Native130/160 objects and source
products in the persistent development database remain the execution starting point; selected AC
acceptance is not reopened by this series.

Physical realization should share source text, reports, computation identities and batch seals.
Project/filter before hydration; capture validated source closure once at its owning scope instead
of reconstructing it per field or metric. Avoid holding database transactions across encoder work.
These remove identifiable repeated work; latency/capacity improvements remain unmeasured.
