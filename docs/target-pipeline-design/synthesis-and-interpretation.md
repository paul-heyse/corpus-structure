# Stream synthesis and interpretation

**Proposed target (L3–L4, 2026-11-01).** This is the long-term design for turning the streams in [the foundation](foundation.md) into grounded records. Notion specifies 84 combinations. It marks the numeric-reference template as prototyped on the reference branch and gives illustrative results, with corpus measurement still pending. The [first production milestone](first-production-milestone.md) narrows the target and governs the initial delivery. [Rollout and evaluation](rollout-and-evaluation.md) owns staged adoption. [L3 · Tiers and combinations](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981bda80be5fe097aa6cc), [L4 · Interpretation layer](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b5836981e2a088e21865905899)

## How the tiers compose

A combination reads observations and already produced annotations, joins on declared keys, and adds a layer without rewriting its inputs. Earlier tiers create the keys and context later tiers consume. Within a tier, each combination's prerequisites still run first. This acyclic production order does not impose a one-way inference order. Related interpretations, such as a unit reading, value role and an antecedent, can update each other within the same bounded inference component even when their outputs are presented in different tiers.

1. **Framing (1.1–1.9).** Establish source spans, block and sentence IDs, section paths and reference IDs on addressed PDF-derived units. Detect language and reliability by block, split within block boundaries, keep captions, footnotes, lists and cells as units, and exclude recognized page furniture from candidate generation. Add section roles, citation and internal-pointer bindings, reference times, exact duplicate groups and edition links. The document and section route chooses the versioned labels, predicate inventory and frames downstream. Exact hashing allows work reuse. Keep near-duplicate and section-role evidence weighted. The proposed stage checks anchor round trips, reviews block and section labels on 20 documents, and confirms duplicates manually. [Tier 1 · Framing](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369812db57fd921cc0f910c)
2. **Span inventory (2.1–2.10).** Reconcile one mention inventory with stable mention IDs, token IDs and dependency heads. Keep typed spans, pronouns and noun phrases, original quantities and their normalized values and units, candidate entity/property attachments, table cells with inherited units and notes, terms and expansions, definitions with their genus, scoped symbol declarations, clause cues, PII flags where relevant, relative-date readings and inferred paragraph/topic boundaries. Use the parse to attach cues and mentions and keep competing interpretations. Type votes from one model family form a dependent group. The proposed stage checks type agreement, reviews 100 quantity attachments and times Stanza on CPU. [Tier 2 · Span inventory](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981f9b526cd13e98c0220)
3. **Identity (3.1–3.8).** Pass inventory mentions to Maverick and ReFinED and align any extra detections back to source spans. Keep singleton clusters when the coreference model omits them. Emit local entity/cluster IDs, KB or seed IDs with competing candidates, typed NIL reasons, and class versus instance distinctions. Resolve links, type and relation compatibility jointly where feasible. Use document-local expansions and definitions, propagate PII flags to coreferent mentions and derive salience and scoped conventions. A local entity can be a valid NIL. Insufficient context, a retrieval failure and genuine ambiguity need separate diagnostics. The proposed stage checks candidate recall and conflicting cluster IDs, reviews NIL reasons and hand-checks 50 clusters. [Tier 3 · Identity](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369815ba14ed8b5098fa57f)
4. **Propositions (4.1–4.9).** Assign proposition IDs and ground each argument through the mention inventory. Align end-to-end relation output after extraction, consider cross-sentence relations via coreference as candidates and compare grounded predicates against mapped KB properties. Assemble table statements from row identity, header/property candidates, typed cell values, notes and conditions. Align cited text values with cells and compare after unit normalization. Fill event roles with entailment features, assemble comparisons from values and cues, and dimension-check formal expressions. The long-term optional atomic-claim pass aligns its propositions to source spans and structured content. Domain/range compatibility, claim grounding, missing content and reviewed text–table disagreements form the stage checks. [Tier 4 · Propositions](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369810f81f1e168b89b607b)
5. **Qualification (5.1–5.11).** Attach polarity, author modality, conditions and exceptions, valid time, genericity, attribution, evidence basis, deontic force and bearer, value role, and stance to the proposition or value span. Use dependency scope, section roles, citation/author contexts, values and soft discourse evidence. Decide a condition's scope jointly with its proposition and numeric references. A qualified claim retains its source spans, coherent alternatives and the four distinct confidence fields. Explicit cues and local attachment are the proposed structured baseline. Implicit or distant qualifications remain partial coverage. The stage proposes a labelled sample of about 200 sentences per qualifier and a generator-disabled baseline for the numeric-reference prototype. [Tier 5 · Qualification](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369816a8312f6ff6cbe4968)
6. **Discourse and argument (6.1–6.7).** Link qualified claims and discourse units as evidence, cause, contrast, concession or elaboration. Track support and attack as separate typed edges, with discourse structure, proximity and NLI as evidence. A support edge can exist without entailment. Classify passage and mention functions and citation intent. Resolve requests, commitments, decisions and approvals to owners and due dates where speaker metadata survived conversion. Pair questions with answers and problems with remedies or solutions. Build an extractive summary from central, grounded claims. The stage checks edge quality on ten documents and pair-candidate recall on independently sampled passages. [Tier 6 · Discourse and argument](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369815e9c55f6b3c8af5074)
7. **Corpus (7.1–7.16).** Build corpus entity IDs and a resolved citation graph using exact keys where available and scored candidates otherwise. Retrieve comparable claims using entity alignment, hybrid search, reranking and entailment evidence. Compare equivalence, entailment and conflict under overlapping qualifiers, conditions, time and scope. Group copies, editions and cited restatements by known origin, retain support and opposition, check citation faithfulness and detect modality or condition drift. Compare editions, merge events into timelines, derive claim topics and novelty, default to one sense until evidence warrants a split, and build terminology and scoped convention mappings. Compare value distributions by property, role and condition band, and join requirements to comparable measurements for compliance findings. The stage proposes 50 reviewed cases each for contradictions, equivalences and compliance, plus spot checks on identity and citation merges. Distinct provenance groups can still share unobserved origins. [Tier 7 · Corpus](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981609797fe6385b80b5c)
8. **Ontology (8.1–8.8).** Propose concepts and distinguish senses, infer candidate hierarchy and instance edges, collect predicate signatures and recurring frame slots, compare kind-level claims with instances in scope, and mine candidate constraints with units and qualifications. Map to reference ontologies and package changes with source spans, provenance groups and affected records. Expert review decides which changes enter the next accepted ontology. Compare the accepted-record value of the new version on held-out documents. [Tier 8 · Ontology](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981608228e6dd588f6b21)
9. **Records and control (9.1–9.6).** Assemble type-specific records from qualified claims, definitions, formulas and event or action roles. Validate applicable shapes, dimensions and formula consistency. Audit entailment, unsupported outputs and missed source material on a sample with independent labels. Route unresolved cases using disagreements, source health, incomplete slots, failed checks and resource limits. Reconciliation and calibration run throughout the tiers. A local CPU LLM can propose residual grounded interpretations with the same acceptance path once evaluation justifies it. Evaluate completeness and precision, utility and resource costs, including the observed rate of LLM calls. [Tier 9 · Records and control](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369816889cad569803d6209)

The [registers](registers/README.md) carry combination IDs, dependencies, join keys, checks and source evidence. The tier descriptions include forward-looking model assignments. For example, the older proposition route proposes a broad paragraph claim pass while the qualification stage establishes its baseline with generation disabled. The first production milestone begins with CPU assembly and narrowly tested specialist routes, with expansion governed by measured gaps.

### Joins that need explicit handling

- **Routed candidates.** Heading class feeds section role, then schema routing, then the mention inventory and the link, coreference and relation passes. The supplied mention set and labels are inputs to model reuse. CQE attachment and parse scope generate quantity/property candidates, while unit dimensions, definitions and local conventions constrain the alternatives. A high-confidence source gate needs a measured operating boundary. Keep the fallbacks for mixed language, uncertain PDF text and metadata missing after rendering. [Framing](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369812db57fd921cc0f910c), [Span inventory](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981f9b526cd13e98c0220)
- **Tables.** Read Docling's cell geometry, including spanning headers, then bind units and notes. Column profiles propose key, dimension and measure roles. Header classification suggests the property, normalizers read value cells, and column type helps link row entities. Joint choices couple property, unit, row type and key columns within the supported table shape. A text mention and a cell qualify as alternate derivations only when their normalized value, role, entity, property and conditions agree. Retain disagreements. [Propositions](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369810f81f1e168b89b607b)
- **Scope and cited evidence.** Keep source-local qualifiers on each assertion before joining across documents. A report with different operating conditions or nonoverlapping valid time can supply a contrast. An explicitly cited assertion must be checked against the cited source when it is available. Track citation intent, attribution and reused evidence separately. Identity merging and independent support are separate decisions. [Qualification](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369816a8312f6ff6cbe4968), [Corpus](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981609797fe6385b80b5c)

## Interpretation components and contracts

The ten target components share the same record and evaluation units. The staged design specifies them from stage 1, starts record acceptance at stage 2, logs escalation from stage 3 and first uses corpus support at stage 7. See the [component register](https://app.notion.com/p/b1966ebe48c6421f8ba6dc3d6446d431) and [stage 1 machinery](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698127bfb2c1a9c18e278b).

| Component | Responsibility and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Record type registry | Defines required and optional roles, record acceptance policy and the value/cost terms for evaluations that use them. |
| Observation store | Retains extractor output as immutable evidence. Identifies source revision, anchor, model and extractor versions, configuration and schema, supplied mentions and context, input digest, output type, run identity, alternatives or abstention, score kind and dependency group. Reuse requires matching complete dependencies. |
| Calibration | Owns fitted evidence transformation and empirical calibration with population, fitting sample and version. Preserves raw scores and their semantics. The 2026-11-03 contract allows raw scores as fitted features and provisional policies where data are sparse. |
| Decision registry | Types each variable and its options, owns candidate generation and its diagnostics, and retains `UNKNOWN` with a separate reason. Distinguishes absent, inapplicable and unresolved fields. A required unresolved decision prevents acceptance of a complete record. |
| Inference template runner | Declares candidate sources, evidence groups, compatibility factors, hard constraints and controlled factor removal. Emits coherent assignments or explicit refusal over state, time or memory limits. |
| Lineage | Represents derivations with their premises, transformations and rule versions, and tracks computation dependencies separately for recomputation. |
| Evaluation harness | Runs independent gold alignment, fixed-evidence and end-to-end comparisons, candidate recall, quality and resource measurements, and uncertainty analysis at the sampling-unit level. |
| Seed ontology | Supplies accepted versioned terms, entities, type hierarchy, signatures, frames and dimensions. Keeps proposals from tier 8 in a review boundary until a later release. |
| Acceptance and escalation | Uses deterministic validation or evaluated probabilistic acceptance. Preserves provisional and unresolved outcomes. The long-term action policy records targeted extraction, region re-conversion or review alongside expected value and cost when those quantities are estimable. |
| Corpus support | Stores qualified supporting and opposing assertions and their dependence groups. M1 retains a ledger. A fitted reliability model would be a separate extension. |

**Observation to record.** A parser can suggest a span, dependency, link, role or quantity. Consolidation interprets such observations under the accepted release. For each local ambiguity, preserve its candidate universe, retrieval scope, exclusions and truncation, then form factors over each connected component. The principal templates cover framing, mention/quantity attachment, numeric reference and value role, identity/type/relation, table header/property/row/value/note, qualifier scope and later corpus comparisons. Explicit compatibility can disallow an assignment when the candidate meanings are established. Noisy links, section roles, discourse and uncertain recognized units remain evidence or decisions. For proposed upstream routing gates, require measured high precision in the relevant population and preserve failure information. See [Principles and contracts](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369813f919ef8685c7ede69), [Decision types](https://app.notion.com/p/bea78039da9b4df887435988c0d1bac4) and [Inference templates](https://app.notion.com/p/2d64c9b112b340509bceb7840f523542).

**Output and change.** Emit accepted records where the declared policy is met, otherwise emit coherent alternatives or a typed unresolved outcome. Keep the original value and any normalization, the anchors for each field, decision and rule references, validation and acceptance status, and model/calibration identity where used. Grounding proves addressability, shape checks prove conformance, and interpretation evaluation assesses fidelity. Derivation lineage lets a reader inspect why an assertion follows. Computation dependencies include rejected or contradictory evidence that influenced the decision and identify which components must be recomputed when an input changes. Reuse the repository's [qualified occurrence, observation and release authorities](../design/DESIGN.md) for those boundaries.

| Confidence field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Author modality | What commitment did the source express, such as “is”, “may” or “must”? |
| Interpretation confidence | How strongly does the evidence support this reading of what the source says? |
| Source or measurement reliability | What is known about the report, measurement or instrument? |
| Corpus evidential support | Which distinct origins support or oppose the qualified proposition, and what dependence is known? |

These quantities remain separate. Several extractors can improve a reading of one statement. The underlying source remains a single assertion occurrence. [Mathematics](mathematics.md) gives the formal model, lineage evaluation and acceptance conditions.

## Worked passage

The Notion example describes a March 2024 test of centrifugal pump P-101 (CP). Its Results section reports 120 m³/h above 2 bar suction pressure, exceeding the 100 m³/h rating in Table 3. A cited earlier work says centrifugal pumps may cavitate below “this pressure” [14]. The evidence groups and downstream corpus cases are illustrative. [Worked example](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698129b75bf8c46cd01965)

| Tier | Result on the example |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Address the report spans, Table 3 and reference [14]. Tag Results, English and born-digital source. |
| 2 | Keep P-101, CP, the pump class, “it” and “this pressure” with spans. Normalize 2 bar to 200 kPa and 120 m³/h to about 0.0333 m³/s. Retain both flows and the condition, comparison and hedge cues. |
| 3 | Resolve “it” to the P-101 instance. Bind its class separately and keep a seed asset match or typed local NIL. Form antecedent alternatives for “this pressure”. |
| 4 | Proposition A is the 120 m³/h test value. B is the 100 m³/h rating, agreeing with the table. The comparison derives 20% above rating from A and B. C is the cited cavitation possibility. |
| 5 | A is a specific measured value, asserted by the report authors based on their test, valid March 2024 under suction pressure greater than 2 bar. B is rated. C is generic, attributed to [14], citation-only and hedged, under suction pressure less than 2 bar. |
| 6 | C and its citation are background. Preserve the directional contrast between A's test condition and C's risk condition. |
| 7 | The hypothetical corpus has 12 statements of C, nine tracing to [14], yielding four known provenance groups. Three lose “may”. The cited work supports the attributed reading. A separate 95 m³/h result at 1.5 bar lies outside A's condition and therefore supplies no contradiction on these premises. |
| 8 | Propose measured flow, rated flow and suction condition slots. Flag the 1.5 bar report for review under C's risk condition. The hedged reported threshold remains a proposed soft expectation. |
| 9 | Assemble and unit-check the grounded measurement record, retain the rating and arithmetic comparison, and preserve C as a qualified claim. The resolved pressure reference needs no additional LLM action in this example. |

The prototype couples the antecedent choice `{q_2bar, q_120, q_100, UNKNOWN}` with each quantity's role `{condition_threshold, measured, rated, UNKNOWN}`. Pint dimensions rule out both flows as pressure antecedents in the fixed-unit example. Proximity initially favors the nearer rating, while “above”, “delivered” and “rating” favor distinct roles. The frame factor rewards choosing an antecedent with the expected threshold role. The template is built at stage 5 even though the resolved referent is shown under tier 3.

| Evidence groups | Illustrative result | Accepted at 0.75 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proximity | 0.51 for the wrong 100 m³/h reading | No |
| Units | 0.50 for 2 bar | No |
| Frames | 0.49 for 2 bar | No |
| Units and proximity | 0.82 for 2 bar | Yes |
| Units and frames | 0.78 for 2 bar | Yes |
| Proximity and frames | 0.54 for 2 bar | No |
| All three | 0.94 for 2 bar | Yes |

The trace reports dividends of +1 for each units-based pair and −1 for the three-way term because the two successful routes overlap. The Shapley shares are approximately 0.67 for units and 0.17 each for proximity and frames. These values describe the reference run using illustrative factors and threshold. The prototype still needs fitted factors, condition-scope choices, a section-role factor and broader antecedent generation such as named operating variables. M1 also keeps ambiguous unit readings as variables so compatibility can recover a lower-scoring correct unit. [Reference example](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b58369814c9058f2d9adbaf84b), [M1 inference contract](https://app.notion.com/p/40d141b5836981e2bdcac2a511da386c)

## Structured routes for eight hard capability families

The long-term proposal first attempts structured assembly over anchored spans. It keeps missing and disputed slots explicit, looks in captions, notes or referenced methods where indicated, and passes only the unresolved decision with candidates and source context to an enabled residual action. Every residual output goes through the same grounding, validation and acceptance contracts. [Non-generative routes](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698127b9a1f535a32f0a29)

| Family | Assemble from available evidence | Cases that remain hard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Findings | Population, setup, intervention/comparator, outcome, effect size, uncertainty, evidence basis and explicit conclusion spans | Unstated causal interpretation |
| Procedures | Ordered action/object graph with tools, inputs, outputs, parameters, preconditions and state changes | Tacit steps or loose narration |
| Conditional rules | Typed conditions, actions and outcomes, retaining exception and priority alternatives | Implicit priorities and complex exceptions |
| Obligations and rights | Bearer, counterparty, action/object, trigger, term and governing clause | Implicit parties or context-dependent force |
| Troubleshooting | Symptom, probable cause, check and remedy from tables and recurring structures | Unstated diagnosis |
| Conventions | Symbol declarations, default units, reference conditions and sign conventions within their document scope | Ambiguous or conflicting declarations |
| Formal statements | Hypotheses, typed objects, operation, conclusion, formula representations and proof reference | General formalization and proof reconstruction |
| Problems and solutions | Problem, givens, method, solution and answer sections | Solving an unresolved problem |

Evaluate a stream against the marginal improvement in complete, grounded records it enables under the chosen operating policy. Retrieval and entailment can supply evidence or a check without independently establishing correctness. See [Rollout and evaluation](rollout-and-evaluation.md) for baseline comparisons.
