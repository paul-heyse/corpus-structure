# Source preparation, seed ontology and model streams

The [first production milestone](first-production-milestone.md) begins at the repository's selected, published Docling + Nemotron PDF product. Prepare linguistic and source-context views once, load the pinned domain release, and run routed GLiNER2.5 operations and deterministic parsers over those views. Preserve their observations, input identities and source addresses for assembly and interpretation. The larger CPU stack and wave schedule below describe expansion candidates and their dependencies. See the [target design index](README.md) and [Notion section 3](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b5836981e8bea1fef28ec39471).

## L0 · Published form, PDF and recognition

**Standing.** The 31 October 2026 Notion decision selects PDF as the only pipeline input, with Docling as the structural front end. How each source reaches its published form, how it is rendered, and how its lost structure is carried forward are Proposed. Repository [ADR-0040](../adr/0040-forward-pdf-only-contracts.md) is the accepted go-forward authority for integrated Docling/RapidOCR and Nemotron 3.5. It places source-to-PDF conversion upstream of repository ingestion. The earlier Notion Nemotron options remain design evidence. [Notion L0](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b58369811b8cd1dc6d9da943b3), [PDF-only decision](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698124bc73ee1447273e70).

### Published-form resolution

Acquire the original source, identify the version a reader would see, and hand that version and a list of lost structures to rendering. For a paper, resolve the final rendered paper and retain the availability of author TeX as separate evidence. For a standard, resolve the issued edition and distinguish drafts. For email, resolve the rendered message while identifying headers, turns and speakers that will be lost. Office revisions and comments are known losses. Rules for Office documents, web snapshots and whether to pass through an existing PDF have yet to be specified. Selection needs an explicit edition/version identity before a digest can represent the chosen source. Historical producers such as PyMuPDF, Trafilatura, docx2python, mail-parser and pylatexenc as original-format text readers describe prior stream roles. Some of those libraries may still have a purpose in rendering or metadata capture, or as utilities on already admitted products. [Published-form resolution](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981558918faf10ad69a8f).

### Rendering to PDF

Produce one PDF from the selected form. Embed all fonts, preserve text as text with usable ToUnicode maps, avoid rasterized text and hidden or occluded text, and pin the renderer and available fonts. System font fallback can alter rendering. Outlined glyphs on otherwise native lines are particularly dangerous because page OCR may suppress an overlapping OCR line, and a poor native encoding can trigger full-page replacement even when surrounding prose is sound. Retain the PDF digest and the determinants of rendering and conversion separately. Conversion reuse depends on the exact PDF, Docling version and effective configuration, including OCR engine, model files, scale, parameters and threads. Renderer, versions and fonts explain the PDF's origin and reproducibility. Pinning reduces variation, while timer-based batching still warrants measurement.

Renderer selection and pre-conversion fidelity checks are open. Post-conversion indicators include encoding damage, full-page replacement, unaccounted vector paths and bitmap overlap. They can flag likely losses, with false positives from decorative images and limitations from fused path boxes. A glyph absent from both text and drawing cannot be recovered by inspecting the converted PDF. [Rendering to PDF](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698182b472d79db82e5996), [Docling health and configuration](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981b9b9bcc79b4053ff15).

### Conversion-time metadata

Capture source-specific evidence while the original and selected form are available. Revisions and annotations need revision ID, author and time. Email needs message ID and speaker/turn information. Author TeX needs formula identity and symbol information, distinguished from recognized LaTeX. Original-format text and geometry may be useful mapping inputs. Capture occurs before Docling assigns pointers, so association is a later operation whose schema, mapping, coverage and failure semantics remain open. The Notion recommendation is to attach captured items as observations keyed to element references, page boxes and intra-element offsets where established. Ambiguous mappings need explicit unresolved outcomes. Dropping the streams loses inputs to framing, provenance and argument analysis. A separate source ingestion path adds cross-representation alignment and conflicts with the selected PDF-only input boundary. [Conversion-time metadata](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698146803ed474d531652f), [metadata decision](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b5836981de810ef76dc4f8027c), [anchor decision](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b5836981778930cbdc26e2b25a).

### Docling configuration and downstream handoff

**Proposed handoff.** Each admitted source view carries its product pin, element/occurrence references and applicable geometry, within-element spans in a named coordinate space, and a map to any derived text view used by a model. Carry section ancestry and reading order across columns and floats, marked page furniture, caption and footnote links, table cells with header roles and spans, formula occurrences with recognized LaTeX where available, and linked conversion-time metadata where supplied. Furniture is excluded from ordinary candidate generation. Caption and note evidence can provide qualifiers and units. Reliability grade `q` and its underlying signals qualify observations, and a region can be reread on request if expected value of information justifies the work. The proposed aggregate grade and calibration need evaluation. The repository's typed native addresses govern existing reads.

**Interface-checked basis.** The Notion research read Docling 2.145.0 source. It records the admitted recipe as RapidOCR on ONNX Runtime, `iso:en`, PP-OCRv6 small, `PDF_AWARE_LAYOUT_REGIONS` and `replace_broken_text_layer=True`. Explicit engine selection preserves comparability across machines. Docling provenance retains page, box and character span. Preserve OCR origin, confidence and geometry through `ObservedRapidOcr` word observations in candidate-page `ocr_regions`. The formula page-lifetime policy refuses `generate_parsed_pages`. Preserve region outcomes and formula outcomes separately from conversion status and coverage.

| Signal | Meaning and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Page `parse_score` | Native encoding damage such as U+FFFD and GLYPH placeholders. Missing glyphs can still yield 1.0. |
| Broken-layer replacement | A zero native score with OCR cells can indicate full-page replacement. The score still describes the original native layer. |
| Page `ocr_score` | Mean confidence of OCR cells surviving the merge. It can be NaN if OCR was skipped or its cells were discarded. |
| OCR share and minimum OCR confidence per element | Describe dependence on OCR and the weakest recognized part. Confidence scales depend on the engine. |
| Unaccounted vector shapes | Evidence of possible path-drawn glyphs after excluding rules. Fused boxes can require inspection of individual paths. |
| Bitmap overlap | Evidence of possible rasterized text. Decorative images can trigger it. |
| Page `layout_score` | Layout uncertainty. Text fidelity requires its own evidence. |

Derive `q` from interpretable component signals. Docling `mean_grade` and `low_grade` average unlike quantities, and the source review found table confidence unimplemented. A calibration proposal samples born-digital pages and compares OCR in `LAYOUT_REGIONS` against native readings by region, font and layout family. That comparison cannot establish recognition accuracy for path-drawn glyphs. Shared recognition damage must inform downstream calibration so several models reading the same damaged input do not multiply its evidential weight. Inline vector math in a native line, some equation-numbered display formulas and math inside table cells can require selected region recognition. Preserve original and alternative readings, alignment and uncertainty. ### Nemotron 3.5 Parse

**Accepted repository target.** Run Docling StandardPdfPipeline with RapidOCR and NVIDIA's true Nemotron triple pass, retaining attributed provider products, typed correspondence, selection, alternatives and coverage behind one integrated product. Preserve the immutable originals and native Docling payload. Governed integration can select, add or replace content in a derived product. Keep conversion, recognition and publication states distinct. M1 consumes the selected product through the existing read boundary. [ADR-0040](../adr/0040-forward-pdf-only-contracts.md), [triple-pass plan](../plans/pdf-docling-pipeline/triple-pass-recognition-and-reconciliation.md), [worker service](../../services/nemotron-parse/README.md).

**Notion design evidence.** The approximately 631M-parameter image parser reads a page or crop per request. True triple pass performs (A) full-page parse, (B) independent layout detection, (C) crop re-parse for Table and Chart regions from the union of both box sets, then merges. Docling's `nemotron_parse_v3_5` preset provides the full-page pass. NVIDIA's reference images use 150 DPI RGB, JPEG q92 for pages and q95 for crops. The Docling preset uses PNG at scale 2.0 with max side 2048. The model card's recommended image bound is 1664×2048. Capture revision, preprocessing, prompts, generation parameters, seed when used, runtime and pass-specific receipts. The reference sampled configuration and greedy serving preset represent different experiments.

A raw parse is a reading-ordered sequence of `<x_X1><y_Y1>BODY<x_X2><y_Y2><class_CLASS>` blocks. It can encode text and furniture, headings, lists, captions, footnotes, references, tables as LaTeX `tabular`, chart types and data tables, formula LaTeX and code. Geometry is at block granularity. Inline math and table cells lack individual boxes and the output has no per-block confidence. The Docling mapping can retain raw body in `orig` while normalizing text. Preserve raw parse, detection and crop outputs with request and merge outcomes so later policy changes can reuse them.

Raw coordinates are fractions of the submitted image. For a crop, map each fraction through the crop offset and dimensions into the page image, then into the product's qualified PDF frame. Check CropBox and rotation, and distinguish top-left image coordinates from PDF item origins. Many-to-many correspondence is ordinary. Geometry, containment, reading order, normalized text and formula context can support alignment. A block overlap or textual formula match grants only the precision established. Docling uses native text plus selected OCR, geometric cell structure and native formula candidates. Nemotron generates text and reading order, and can expose chart data and inline math with coarser geometry. Its hallucination, omissions, repetition and truncation modes are distinct from Docling's OCR and selection failures.

Retain `finish_reason`, token usage, malformed or incomplete blocks, unknown classes, crop failures and merge decisions. Truncation can occur at 8,744 tokens per parse request. Token log probabilities, if available, are model signals. Text, order, table and formula agreement are computed evidence. The Notion options compare reference orchestration in a side environment, a root HTTP client reusing NVIDIA algorithms, Docling engine reuse and the single-pass preset. They also compare dedicated versus shared runtimes, greedy versus sampled decoding, model revisions and raster strategies, full-page versus selected-region scope, add-only versus guarded/exclusive replacement, raw versus mapped product storage, and evidence-only, gap-fill or coverage-audit use. The old comparison was neutral. ADR-0040 owns repository selection and limits downstream responsibility.

**Measured externally.** The vendor's ParseBench report, cited by Notion, compared triple+union (75.20 at 10.62 pages/s) with a single pass (72.74 at 15.42 pages/s) on 2,078 pages and an H100. Transfer to the local GPU and corpus requires separate evidence. Two requests per page plus each selected table/chart crop amplify work and raw retention. Relevant unknowns in the Notion research include local attention and MTP behavior, memory/concurrency, token log probabilities, runtime compatibility, revision and decode effects, merge ordering, geometry correspondence, chart mapping, rotation and local quality. The repository service records an RTX 5090 startup and selected-page controls on 31 October 2026 with effective FlashAttention 2 and one-token MTP. It gives the shared GPU back to Standard Docling for its phase through the dedicated service's sleep/wake controls. Those observations settle specific local runtime questions. Current-source full-book publication and consumer acceptance are tracked separately. [Worker service and local evidence](../../services/nemotron-parse/README.md), [Nemotron options and unknowns](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981c9b813f5202cf5dd30), [triple-pass reference](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b58369810a9907f9dbbef94efe).

### Failure and escalation

Record rendering failure distinctly before ingestion, and preserve acquired inputs. The Notion design leaves retry policy and admission of partly converted documents open. For PDF conversion and region recognition, keep lifecycle status, structural validity, native-text quality, recognition outcome, coverage and alignment separate. The repository has `PdfConversionOutcome` (`success`, `partial_success`, `failure`), `PdfCoverage` (`complete`, `partial`, `unknown`, `unsupported`), per-formula outcomes, OCR region outcomes and unresolved `PdfInlineAlignmentReport` rows. A region can be partial when validated words do not cover its text or word boxes fail validation. Unresolved alignment carries source pointers and the basis for uncertainty. Keep the worker isolated from database handles and use existing cancellation and task ownership. Missing layout clusters, disabled OCR, no raster renderer and full-page replacement can degrade text without a fatal conversion error.

For scans, the current PDF-aware route recognizes layout-selected regions, which can miss text outside clusters. The Notion alternative is a separate `FULL_PAGE` conversion for scanned documents, raising RapidOCR `Global.max_side_len` above its 2,000 px default to avoid shrinking A4 at scale 3 to roughly 170 DPI. The mode applies to the whole conversion and skips native font/cell decoding. Page-specific routing for mixed documents and handling rotated scans remain open. `ObservedRapidOcr` refuses the proposed `detect_orientation` configuration. An existing invisible OCR layer can be reintroduced where its cells do not overlap new OCR, so provenance matters. For a consequential uncertain region, request selective rereading with an appropriate OCR/formula/table recognizer, retain both readings and qualify engine-specific confidences and correspondence. Escalate unresolvable cases or leave the relevant output partial. [Failure handling](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698173977ced93021aac08), [Docling configuration](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981b9b9bcc79b4053ff15).

## L1 · Versioned seed ontology

**Notion target, Proposed in this package.** Load accepted ontology `O` once per run before tier 1 (wave 0). Pin its version and accepted concept IDs on interpretations made under it. It supplies concepts, definitions, aliases, known domain entities, hierarchy, relation signatures and direction, frame and event roles, quantity dimensions, unit conventions, logical relationships, typical ranges and coverage expectations. In identity resolution, local domain targets supplement any external KB. A term such as P-101 can match an accepted local asset or retain NIL with a reason. Concept, entity, formula symbol and assertion role remain distinct identities. The choice of v0 and its input format are open. The existing domain pack is the starting repository authority and must be mapped explicitly to the required content.

In `P(z | e, O)`, compile selected ontology content according to meaning. Adopted scoped constraints can exclude an invalid interpretation, logical rules can derive conclusions from accepted premises, and defeasible expectations can contribute compatibility with uncertainty. Preserve unknown concepts, unbound roles and missing values as candidates or explicit states. A typical range is weak evidence against a surprising measurement. OWL domain/range can entail types, whereas SHACL shapes validate a data graph. The proposal includes OWL-RL and pySHACL for these separate roles. An ontology edge by itself establishes neither conditional independence nor a probabilistic dependency. The existing finite inference and ontology validation routes have their own scope.

Maintain separate authority for the accepted version, interpretations pinned to it, and tier-8 proposals. Induced concepts, aliases, edges, signatures, frames and rules retain proposed identities and evidence until review. Acceptance assigns the concept its accepted identity in the next release. Replay affected interpretations under the new release. Proposals cannot supply confirmation to the interpretations that produced them. Compare plausible wrong expectations in evaluation so an attractive prior cannot silently erase contradictory evidence. [Seed ontology](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981b796e0e48ade290791), [tiers and interpretation](synthesis-and-interpretation.md).

## L2 · Model streams

### Stream contract and keys

**Proposed.** A stream is an immutable, keyed layer of observations from a model or deterministic producer. Later joins write separate layers. A reusable result binds producer and operation versions, schema/labels, model and tokenizer/checkpoint, source view and exact span, supplied mentions, context membership and windows, effective preprocessing and decoding, and any ontology or calibration version it actually consumes. Changes to labels, candidates or context can trigger targeted replay while unchanged layers remain reusable. Distinguish a cached model invocation from its attributed observations and from interpreted decisions. Preserve run coverage and terminal outcomes even for empty results. Track shared invocation, checkpoint, input hints and duplicate dependencies to avoid treating correlated observations as independent. M1 uses the existing method, reuse and publication boundaries for these obligations. [Notion L2](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b5836981ba9c27d94c97f087da), [repository method contract](../design/D4-method-catalog.md#1-the-method-contract).

| Join key | Producer or owner in the target | Downstream use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontology version and accepted concept ID | L1 release | Version interpretation and resolve local concepts, roles, relation signatures and units |
| Character offset with a source map | Canonical text derived from the PDF product | Ground output in its text view and resolve back to page/box/font where established |
| Block ID, section path and table/cell or formula occurrence ID | Docling structure and admitted PDF product | Join captions, notes, headings, cells and formula symbol positions |
| Reference ID, citation marker and DOI | GROBID | Resolve citations and group provenance |
| Sentence ID | SaT or routed math-aware splitter in the wider design | Share segmentation across encoders, claims and entailment |
| Token ID and dependency head | Stanza in the wider design | Scope qualifiers, find the definition genus, attach values and propose mentions |
| Mention ID | Tier-2 inventory, including typed mentions and parse-derived candidates | Supply entity linking/coreference mentions and align relation arguments |
| Cluster ID | Maverick with supplied mentions | Rank and resolve entities across sentences |
| Wikidata ID or NIL with fine type | ReFinED, local KB subset and accepted domain targets | Type-check relations, corroborate links and seed corpus identity |
| Interpretation ID and candidate set | L4 decision owner | Revise a judgment while retaining observations, alternatives and an unresolved state |
| Proposition ID | Relations and claim assembly | Attach qualifiers, compare claims and build argument links |
| Registry ID and claim-group ID | Splink and claim equivalence | Aggregate identity, support and novelty across sources |
| Directed corpus citation edge | Corpus citation graph | Identify provenance groups and test attributed support |
| Proposed concept ID | Tier-8 concept formation | Carry proposed structure and evidence to review and later release |

Use those IDs as authority in M1, with reversible maps to linguistic views. The [register inventory](registers/README.md) preserves individual stream keys and dependency rows.

### Run plan and leverage

**Proposed execution order for the full target.** The dependency graph includes both model inputs and combination inputs. The Notion topological sort assigns earliest waves 0–25 in five overlapping phases. Wave order determines data availability. Decisions within a bounded component can still be solved jointly.

| Phase | Waves | Work and cost control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · Per document | 0–3 | Load the accepted ontology once, ingest the PDF, emit layout, citations, segmentation, language, quality and duplicate groups, then embeddings/keyword index and early framing. Exact duplicate blocks can share model work while preserving occurrence identities. |
| B · Parallel local extraction | 4–10 | Syntax, heading/document classes, quantities, applicable PII, discourse, routed GLiNER/bi-encoder, table assembly, then identity and relations given the mention inventory. Gated claim extraction in paragraph windows is the high-volume generative branch. |
| C · Joins and local inference | 5–18 | Span inventory, candidate generation, bounded joint inference, propositions and qualification. Re-extract specific unresolved roles. Oversize components enter difficulty routing. |
| D · Corpus | 12–22 | Registry, citation graph, sense inventory, equivalence, contradiction, support, drift, faithfulness, editions, events, topics, distributions and compliance. Score retrieved candidate pairs. |
| E · Ontology and records | 15–25 | Stage proposed changes separately, assemble compatible assignments, validate, route difficulty and evaluate generative fallback on unresolved slots. |

The broader critical path runs heading classes → section roles → schema routing → GLiNER label sets → mention inventory → linking, coreference and relations. Generic labels can run before headings are available. A targeted second pass adds routed labels under a distinct input key. Major expected CPU pressures are Stanza, Maverick, ReFinED and the claim LLM. Concurrent encoder work must share one capacity policy and avoid multiplying parallelism across worker pools. **Computed graph evidence.** Reach counts the 84 combinations that depend on a stream directly or transitively, through execution or interpretation dependencies. Models conditioned counts later models whose routing, gating, labels or inputs the stream affects. The audit shows high reach for layout (83), citations (82), sentences (81), language (78), syntax (73) and document/heading classes (72). Cheap gates can inflate reach. Pair scorers can have low reach and still determine late equivalence, contradiction or faithfulness. Deduplication, language, block type and reference gating reduce the cost of the wider LLM path.
