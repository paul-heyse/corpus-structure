# Dispositions of the consumed audit findings (supporting document)

**Role.** One disposition per finding of the refreshed audit consumed by the design review
([review.md](../design_review_unified-domain-model-and-execution_2026-11-18.md)). The audit (refreshed 2026-11-18 at `d78c98765`, with the independent
challenger's corrections of the same date) owns the diagnoses; this page says what the review's
target does with each. Disposition vocabulary: **corrected by the target**, **changed** (the target
alters the finding's premise or remedy), **retained with reason**, **deferred with trigger**.
Current execution status, once scheduled, belongs to the plan created from the review, not here.
All remedies are Proposed.

**Revised 2026-11-18.** The dispositions below were revised by an independent recommendation audit
from input revision `b4eb74f67`, on the operator's concern that the target should represent the
pipeline's intentional flexibility rather than govern it away. Rows DA-070, DA-072, DA-073, DA-074,
DA-084, DA-085 and DA-086 changed; the
[audit companion](../audits/design_review_unified-domain-model-and-execution_2026-11-18_audit.md)
explains each change. The first reviewer did not author the revised dispositions.

DA-087 was merged into DA-081 by the challenger (one cause: no typed registry of programs and
generated outputs); its content is disposed under DA-081. The conversion of the remaining
hand-written PostgreSQL is scheduled open work under ADR-0131 and the DDO coordinator; DA-084 is
disposed on that basis.
