## Material changes and their consequences

**Candidate retention and partial knowledge.** Select raw overlapping Doc.spans groups over retained
views, with existing component provenance. Do not use Doc.ents/filter_spans as the authoritative
candidate universe. Keep missing/outside/blocked NER annotations specific to their component; original
source opportunities survive token unalignment and incomplete annotation. Optional preset-span
classification uses separate raw/output groups and suitable independent supervision.

**Parser provenance.** Select lexer-only Lark where lexical evidence suffices and prepared routed
grammars otherwise. TextSlice coordinates refer to its actual buffer, with basic/contextual lexer
limits and possible repeated prefix work. Token normalization and ordinary copy/serialization have
different provenance contracts. Lower consumed nodes into existing immutable located products at the
actual boundary. Syntactic ranking is neither semantic support nor a posterior probability.

**Probabilistic modeling beyond backend substitution.** Select the real factor policy now. Conditional
small-case independence checks, prior-regularized discrete fitting and empirical-marginal fitting can
improve that model without a solver replacement. They neither create independent data nor remove
double-counting automatically. Tagged DiscreteEM drops partially missing rows; mirror descent fits
selected empirical marginals from data, not unrestricted external arrays. MPLP's objective gap is
not posterior uncertainty. Causal/linear-Gaussian mechanisms need their own justified scientific model.

**Estimator and identity composition.** Select event-specific baseline/fold analysis and concrete
pilot contracts now. Reusable neighbor graphs and frozen state retain population/metric/fold/training
identity. Native Splink evaluation and external-pair scoring avoid unnecessary custom machinery;
array blocking can challenge candidate omissions. Numerical clusters and match scores cannot
authorize canonical merges. The revised principal retains exact API, tagged/installed-source and
addendum references beside each mechanism.

**Better action on the improved-M1 input.** The original §9 crosswalk remains in the principal.
Its witness, composition, real-policy and source-expansion tracks now name concrete native
realizations and countercases. Design the small experiments and their event/population now; actual
fitting and claims wait for suitable data. Dossier optimization cannot repair membership, and a new
solver cannot supply missing witnesses or factors.
