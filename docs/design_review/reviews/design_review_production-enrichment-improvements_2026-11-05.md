## 2. Responsibilities, dependencies and semantic ownership

The present model has real authority over objects, relations, operations, finite lifecycles and
effects. The gap exposed by F37 is more specific: `methods/definition_applicability.py:108–157`
constructs a passage-pair classification and decodes a category plus passage IDs and generic reason.
It does not locate the particular term, defining expression or usage-to-definition compatibility.
The historical automatic-quality record reports eight wrong pair judgments among twenty inspected
pairs; manual supplied-product support is a separate result. Those errors are exposed-development
classification evidence, not proof that publication admitted unsupported claims.

cardinality and operation signatures; existing domain functions own extraction, scoped attachment,
comparison and selection algorithms. Preparation lowers the same definitions into typed boundaries
and native products. Orchestration connects these operations without classifying their semantics.
Evaluator-side reference data remains inaccessible to producers; native eligibility owns admission.

## 5. Change and failure scenarios

A renamed defined-term role changes its declaration/binding, not independent producer/read classifiers.
Two dependency variants with different RIGHT_ID order retain distinct mapping. Passive actor/object
roles do not inherit surface order. Missing retained DEP yields truthful unavailability, not parser fallback.

A term is correctly defined in a target while its source usage is ambiguous: retain the target component,
withhold usage applicability. A passage explains one term and mentions another: assess each relation
at its own occurrence. Two compatible relationship kinds survive selection; two incompatible senses
remain alternatives. A changed source version invalidates affected compatibility without re-encoding
unrelated immutable inventory vectors.

A nested header supplies a unit only to its band; note markers govern selected rows. Unstated header
facets stay distinct from missed positive attachments. The same glyph in adjacent examples retains
separate scope. Clause revisions preserve both versions, comparator direction and endpoint inclusion.
Dossier selection cannot discard a conflict to increase apparent coverage.

Enterprise glossary/procedure/revision examples are a small proposed transfer packet with scoped
bindings. Select one producing domain per operation and explicit cross-source comparison projections;
no general simultaneous multi-pack runtime is needed. Replacing the GLiNER classifier with rule-based
witnesses leaves the component contract and assessment question stable; changing the exact solver
requires equivalent event/alternative/refusal semantics, not merely a matching MAP assignment.
