| item | decision | guideline_gap | reason |

| claim c_S_def, statement_role (A definiens, B none) | keep B (none) | yes | Same as T3 c_aX_def: §10.3 names derived variables (defined_as) in a theorem without a role; definiens is used for stipulated formulas in definitions. The guideline does not settle the role |

| claim c_S_normal, evidence (A: s3 and s4 supporting; B: s3 and s4 primary) | keep B (s3, s4 primary) | no | The parameter values in s3 and s4 are part of the has_distribution claim (§10.8). Support would understate them |
