## Text
[s1] Suppose that \(Y\) has the normal distribution with mean \(\mu \in \R\) and standard deviation \(\sigma \in (0, \infty)\).
[s2] Then \(X = e^Y\) has the lognormal distribution with parameters \(\mu\) and \(\sigma\).
[s3] (a) The parameter \( \sigma \) is the shape parameter of the distribution.
[s4] (b) The parameter \( e^\mu\) is the scale parameter of the distribution.
[s5] If \(Z\) has the standard normal distribution then \(W = e^Z\) has the standard lognormal distribution.
