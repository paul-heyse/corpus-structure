## Guesses

1. Standing context not annotated (§10.2). [c1]–[c4] are outside the unit.
2. Indexed family and independence (§10.4, §10.7). The independence of (X_1, …, X_n) is a condition predicate with arity 2 or more. An n-term sequence cannot be written that way, so I used X_1 and X_n as the two arguments, both bound to the indexed entity. This does not say the sequence is mutually independent, and the condition stays in the tree of the conclusion. Treat the representation as an approximation.
3. Parameter canonical names (§10.4). The packet does not name the role of σ, so I used the symbols `\mu` and `\sigma` as canonical names. In T4, σ is named `shape` from its role sentence. The same lognormal parameter therefore has two canonical names in two units. Cross-unit identity needs settling.
4. "Where" equations (§10.3, §10.5). "where μ = Σμ_i and σ² = Σσ_i²" are recorded as unsupported (u1). defined_as is for random variables only, and derived_parameter is for family parameters. One could also record them as cmp equations in the conclusion's condition tree; I did not, because they state values rather than restrictions.
5. Premise restrictions (§10.7). The restrictions μ_i ∈ R and σ_i ∈ (0, ∞) are a forall node over i in the conclusion's tree, with the premise claim as a claim node.
6. Proof (§10.3, §10.2). Steps are not annotated. s3's "from definition [1]" is a proof step, so not recorded. The proof statement is kept with its proves claim.
7. Theorem anchors. s1–s2 for the theorem, s3–s5 for the proof, by sentence id.
