## Guesses

1. Statement split (§10.2, §10.3). The packet header says "paragraph + unit" and gives no statement kind. I treated s1–s3 as a narrative paragraph and s4–s6 as one definition. Basis: the rendition offsets (paragraph 41–436, unit 450–744) and the "is called" wording. I annotated s3 as an `assumption` (its "we assume" supposition) and s4–s6 as a `definition`.
2. s1–s2 (probability space, Ω, F, P as notation) not captured. §10.3 keeps only explicit claims from narrative text, and I judged these as set-up or notation.
3. Z has section extent (§10.4). "In this section, we assume that we fixed random variable Z" is not one of the guideline's example binding phrases ("Suppose again", "Throughout this section"); I took it as a section binding by analogy.
4. Parameter canonical names (§10.4). The role words "location" and "scale" are stated in s6, after a and b are introduced (s4). The guideline says to use the role word the source states and the symbol only when none is stated, but does not say what to do when the role comes after the introduction within the same unit. I used the role words.
5. Binding kind for a and b inside the formulas (§10.4 vs D1 §6.1). I used `family_param` (they are FamilyParameter entities). `free` with restriction conditions was the other reading.
6. Modality of "let X = a + bZ" (§10.6). §10.6 says "let" introduces `supposed` premises, but here the sentence is a definition's stipulation. I used `defined`.
7. Premise claim in the condition tree (§10.7). The defined_as and construction_formula conditions reference claim c1 (Z takes values in R), since the definition presupposes it. Whether a premise stated in a different statement belongs in the condition tree is not settled.
8. param_symbols qualifier on construction_formula (§10.3 lists formulas but not this qualifier). Its meaning (symbol to parameter entity, or to canonical name) is not defined, so I left it out. The symbols map on the formula carries the bindings.
9. Role claims. parameter_role and has_parameter are recorded for a and b (c5–c8) even though the canonical names already carry the role. I read the guideline as wanting these claims when a role is stated.
10. Construction, not family (§10.4). I used FamilyConstruction for "location-scale family associated with …", as §10.4 gives as the example.

## What the schema could not express

- The alias kinds (label, symbol, term) are not carried: the validator accepts aliases only as strings, so the kind is dropped.
- The schema lists `symbol` as an identity slot of the schema's `RandomVariable`, but the class has no identity key for it, so I kept the symbol as a plain field.
- Entity-valued identity slots (family in FamilyParameter identity): the format does not give the shape, so I used `{"ref": ...}`.
