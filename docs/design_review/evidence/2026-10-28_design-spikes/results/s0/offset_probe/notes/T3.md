# T3 notes (The Normal Distribution, affine transformation)

## Guesses
- Statement kind: unlabelled result; I used `theorem` (could be `proposition`).
- Proof: the `Details:` block is a `proof` statement with `proves` -> theorem (§10.2). With
  anchors fixed to `"unit"`, the proof and the theorem share an anchor. Evidence for `proves` cites
  `Details:` (primary) and the proof's concluding clause (supporting); the guideline does not say
  what span a `proves` claim cites.
- Derived variable (§10.3): `a + b X` is a local RandomVariable with `defined_as a + b X`. Modality
  `asserted` (default) and no `statement_role` because none of the five roles fits; the guideline
  gives no modality for a derived variable in a theorem.
- Bindings: in the conclusion, `mu` is `param_of` the premise's mean, `sigma` is
  `param_expr_of` the premise's variance, `a` and `b` are `free`, restricted by the condition
  tree.
- Premise X has statement scope ("Suppose that", not "again"), so `e_X` extent `statement`.
- No FamilyParameter entities created: `param_values` keys are canonical names (mean,
  variance) and no claim is about the parameters themselves.
