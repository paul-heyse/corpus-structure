## Timing

- baseline edited-noovr.tokenizer: 17862 uncached parses, median 42 ms, p99 382 ms, max 3150 ms; parse timeouts 0; spans whose resolver hit its bound 3.
- extended.tokenizer: 17836 uncached parses, median 85 ms, p99 859 ms, max 5804 ms; parse timeouts 5; spans whose resolver hit its bound 5.
- **Run-to-run variation at the timer.** The two final runs (same grammar; they differ only in the tree printer, which does not affect status) differ in status on 3 spans, all long display spans near the 2 s timer (ambiguous/failed, failed/ok, ok/failed); with 30 parallel workers parse times are load-dependent. Figures above are from `extended.tokenizer`; the step-3 trials treat a read span that took >= 1.5 s and then times out as a timing effect, not a break (counted per trial in `extensions.json`). The extensions roughly double the median parse time (Earley over more rules).
- **Resolver bound (finding for the port).** `api._Resolver` merges readings by converting each to SymPy outside the parse timer; on the first full run three workers spent more than 11 minutes in mpmath quadrature reached from `transformer.eq` (`Eq(...)` evaluating an integral numerically). S5 bounds the conversion (1 s per reading, 4 s per value); a reading not converted in time is not comparable, so the value stays `ambiguous`, never picked. The port needs one deadline over the whole read (parse, resolver, occurrences), or unevaluated relations in the resolver.
