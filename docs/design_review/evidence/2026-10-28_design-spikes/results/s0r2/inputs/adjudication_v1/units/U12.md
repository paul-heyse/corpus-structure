[c1] Suppose that \( X \) has the standard gamma distribution with shape parameter \( k \in (0, \infty) \).

## Text
[s1] The mean and variance of \( X \) are
[s2] (a) \(\E(X) = k\)
[s3] (b) \(\var(X) = k\)
--- Details: ---
[s4] (a) From [3], \[ \E(X) = \int_0^\infty x \frac{1}{\Gamma(k)} x^{k-1} e^{-x} \, dx = \frac{\Gamma(k + 1)}{\Gamma(k)} = k\]
[s5] (b) From [3] again \[ \E\left(X^2\right) = \int_0^\infty x^2 \frac{1}{\Gamma(k)} x^{k-1} e^{-x} \, dx = \frac{\Gamma(k + 2)}{\Gamma(k)} = (k + 1) k\] and hence \( \var(X) = \E\left(X^2\right) - [\E(X)]^2 = k \)
