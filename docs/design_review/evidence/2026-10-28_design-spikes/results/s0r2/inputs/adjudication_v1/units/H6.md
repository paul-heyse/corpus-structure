## Text
[s1] Suppose that the income of a certain population has the Pareto distribution with shape parameter 3 and scale parameter 1000.
[s2] Find each of the following:
[s3] (a) The proportion of the population with incomes between 2000 and 4000.
[s4] (b) The median income.
[s5] (c) The first and third quartiles and the interquartile range.
[s6] (d)
[s7] The mean income.
[s8] (e) The standard deviation of income.
[s9] (f) The 90th percentile.
--- Details: ---
[s10] (a) \(\P(2000 \lt X \lt 4000) = 0.1637\)
[s11] so the proportion is 16.37%
[s12] (b) \(Q_2 = 1259.92\)
[s13] (c) \(Q_1 = 1100.64\), \(Q_3 = 1587.40\), \(Q_3 - Q_1 = 486.76\)
[s14] (d) \(\E(X) = 1500\)
[s15] (e) \(\sd(X) = 866.03\)
[s16] (f) \(F^{-1}(0.9) = 2154.43\)
