## Text
[s1] The basic Pareto distribution with shape parameter \(a \in (0, \infty)\) is a continuous distribution on \( [1, \infty) \) with distribution function \( G \) given by \[ G(z) = 1 - \frac{1}{z^a}, \quad z \in [1, \infty) \]
[s2] The special case \( a = 1 \) gives the standard Pareto distribution.
--- Details: ---
[s3] Clearly \( G \) is increasing and continuous on \( [1, \infty) \), with \( G(1) = 0 \) and \( G(z) \to 1 \) as \( z \to \infty \).
