## Text
[s1] A metal rod is designed to fit into a circular hole on a certain assembly.
[s2] The radius of the rod is normally distributed with mean 1 cm and standard deviation 0.002 cm.
[s3] The radius of the hole is normally distributed with mean 1.01 cm and standard deviation 0.003 cm.
[s4] The machining processes that produce the rod and the hole are independent.
[s5] Find the probability that the rod is to big for the hole.
--- Details: ---
[s6] Let \(X\) denote the radius of the rod and \(Y\) the radius of the hole.
[s7] \(\P(Y - X \lt 0) = 0.0028\)
