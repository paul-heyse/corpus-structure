## Public eligibility boundary

The native [local semantic policy](../../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/sql/record_publication.sql)
accepts only a directly anchored contextual relationship proposal under its
specific question, rubric, context policy and protected case. A predicate
qualification is an anchorless directed relation to exact typed components.
The host frame's physical predicate anchor belongs to its declared member,
not to a fabricated relation occurrence. The
[structural publication](../../../../python/corpus-structure/src/corpus_structure/semantic/structural_admission.py)
policies also have no condition/host statement kind. Neither route can admit
the relation as currently declared.

The next public contract needs one released directed statement projection,
exact qualifier anchors and host predicate-member anchors under their
retained endpoint roles, and a scope/effective-version binding. Reuse the
existing protected source-operation evaluator where it represents that
projection, with independent cases authoring the correct host, relation, and
meaning plus wrong, ambiguous and absent alternatives. Native eligibility
and atomic publication must verify the same role-bound statement and
adjudicated case; successful sibling parts must be independently eligible
even when another part is limited. A model no-match and a deterministic
private attachment are separate results. Do not substitute a contextual
relation label, set an artificial occurrence ID, or use a producer decision
as admission evidence.
