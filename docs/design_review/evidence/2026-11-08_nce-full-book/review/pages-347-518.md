- **Caption survival and identity.** The original Figure 9.9 on physical page 354 is three panels with one shared caption. Standard projects that caption from `#/texts/6650`. Combined exposes three native pictures or charts and chart cells, and the caption is absent from its compared projection and native item labels. Figure 11.6 on page 470 shows the same loss in combined. Standard projects its caption from `#/texts/8629`. On page 477 the direction of error reverses, with one visible Figure 11.8 caption projected twice by combined, from `#/texts/2963` and `#/texts/6287`.
- **Native content can survive a projection error.** The original Table 11.1 on physical page 416 shows six Land of Oz transition matrices. Both retained native formula items contain values in their `orig` readings, with Standard `#/texts/7745` and combined `#/texts/2550`. Both selected formula projections are only the incomplete string `\begin{array} {`. The displayed maze transition matrix on page 448 has the same symptom, as do selected matrix projections on pages 462 and 468. A page census based only on nonempty projections misses these losses.
- **Mathematical damage can survive formula recognition.** The printed equal-probability gambler’s ruin case on physical page 439 gives the fraction i/N. Combined `#/texts/8715` projects a malformed subscript-like expression instead, and `#/texts/8716` loses multiplication structure in the absorption recurrence. Standard retains the explicit fraction and cleaner recurrence in the inspected passages. Formula item counts alone conceal the difference.
- **Table dimensions and cell accuracy.** The finite-case chain-letter table on physical page 398 declares 21 rows and 13 columns in both products. In combined `#/tables/17`, the second data row lacks the original Profit value 250. Negative Profit values from the first and third rows are in zero-based column 11 rather than column 12, replacing the source’s Z12 zero. The Standard values in `#/tables/46` match the inspected early cells. Appendix A on physical page 508 is a visible normal-distribution grid. Both products retain its diagram and unstructured text in lieu of a native table, including `04` and `3264` where the PDF shows `.04` and `.3264`. Appendix B on physical page 509 is printed sideways. Standard `#/tables/51` and combined `#/tables/6` both put the upright table’s rightmost medians at the first native row and its left-hand height heading at the final native row.
- **The combined arm also recovers meaningful structure.** Appendix C on physical page 510 presents two four-column life-table panels. Standard `#/tables/52` has a 45 × 7 grid, combining age 43 and its “Both sexes” count 94707 in one cell and merging the final two source rows. The checked source values include both opening and ending rows.
- **Index structure needs a separate check.** Physical pages 511–518 have two columns of alphabetic references. Both arms preserve much of their wording while switching among index tables, generic tables, ordinary text blocks, and individual references. The original pages 511, 512, and 515 confirm column structure. The source column order and entry boundaries require their own comparison.
