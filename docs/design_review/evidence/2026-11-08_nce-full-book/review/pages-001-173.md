The independent naming-convention evaluation on pages 151 and 173 found omitted conditions and an unresolved reported proposition in the downstream scoped comparison. The separate condition omission in the protected comparison remains a downstream semantic failure.

The exact findings above are useful improvement targets for preserving math glyph semantics, keeping object-to-caption relations through reconciliation, separating graphical reconstruction from source text, resolving inherited page locators, and detecting formula output that repeats outside its source region. These are development findings. The page census and targeted checks do not establish line-by-line semantic fidelity, correct chart values, all table cells, every relationship, or the quality of every downstream method output.
