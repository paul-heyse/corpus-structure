## Combined judgment

Retain the useful model/Core/PostgreSQL/driver/Arrow composition for existing consumers, while removing premature choices that the initial addendum-focused review made. In particular, do not preselect custom native-object management without comparing established complete libraries, and do not select a second ADBC reader simply because bound queries are available.

Original features broaden the improvement beyond small adapters: typed stable relational preparation, a complete native-object lifecycle comparison, typed columnar published-data access, operator database description and selected-subtree streaming exports. These compose with the additions but are not all newly documented features. Their value and prerequisites remain distinct; they are not a required expansion of PDF acceptance.

The audit also corrects stale implementation descriptions. Current record readers, export cursors, source-reader checkout scoping and retained-envelope decomposition landed after the original review. Recommending their initial delivery again would misdescribe current work. Conversely, new orchestration call sites alone do not establish that all publication prerequisites exist.
