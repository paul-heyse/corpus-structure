# Ownership guideline

State only the ownership text actually supplies. Identify organizations by registry ID, keeping
case. Preserve the stated validity intervals, shares and cited sources. Filing references carry
provenance without changing the fact. An informational notice is not an extraction task.

For an inspection, represent the asset's `inspection` relation using the declared nested
`InspectionValue`. Preserve the inspector and custodian as separate organization roles, the
observed condition, the measured quantity's exact decimal, unit and stated basis, and the
inspection date. Cite the source sentences that establish those details. Repeated reports stay
separate source occurrences even when they support the same ownership fact.
